### [1] [Predicting protein structures at any folding stage from sequence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04287)
*Lisheng Zhang,Qingsong Li,Zinan Xie,Yanting Yang,Qikui Yang,Peng Lu,Zhican Fu,Wentao Cui,Runtong Hou,Zhengwei Xie*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: FoldScape是一个序列依赖的深度学习框架，能生成特定折叠分数Q下的Cα构象集合，覆盖折叠过程中更多的中间态，与AlphaFold 3互补。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有蛋白质结构预测器仅描述天然构象，无法展示从非折叠到折叠态的连续过程。FoldScape旨在填补这一空白。

Method: 通过层次化滑动窗口回归一维残基间距离，结合链组装和修正流解码器采样，基于多尺度粗粒化折叠轨迹和全原子机械展开轨迹训练。

Result: 在20种人类蛋白质中，FoldScape覆盖的折叠坐标比AlphaFold 3多一个数量级，重构精度在折叠全程保持稳定。

Conclusion: FoldScape无需单蛋白模拟，即可稳定覆盖折叠过程的多个阶段，并与AlphaFold 3形成互补。

Abstract: Protein structure predictors reliably identify native conformations, but do not describe the continuum connecting unfolded ensembles to folded states. Here we present FoldScape, a sequence-conditioned deep learning framework that generates C$α$ conformational ensembles at a specified folding fraction $Q$. FoldScape reconstructs three-dimensional chains through hierarchical, sliding-window regressions of one-dimensional inter-residue distances, followed by chain assembly and sampling with a rectified-flow decoder. It is trained on multi-scale coarse-grained folding trajectories and all-atom mechanical-unfolding trajectories. Across 20 human proteins (36-857 residues), at matched sample size, FoldScape covers about an order of magnitude more of the folding coordinate than seed-varied AlphaFold 3 ensembles, robustly across proteins, order parameters and binning choices. AlphaFold 3 better anchors the immediate native vicinity, which FoldScape does not reach, making the approaches complementary. FoldScape reconstruction accuracy remains approximately constant along $Q$, from collapsed coil to near-native globule, rather than deteriorating away from the native state. A stride-one crop-and-superpose scheme extends inference to chains several times longer than a single chain-level forward pass. We also show that training trajectories resolve mechanical unfolding at residue resolution, that elongation-conditioned ensembles exhibit reproducible continuous observables across pulling velocities, and that partially unfolded chains form geometrically coherent segments. FoldScape thus combines broad coverage of folding stages with stable reconstruction accuracy, without per-protein simulation.

### [2] [Ablating Design Choices for Protein Ensemble Generators Across Structural Regimes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05055)
*Eoin Quinn,Oliver Bent*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 论文研究了蛋白质构象集合生成模型的设计选择在不同结构（有序、无序、多域和过渡蛋白）中的适用性，发现训练数据而非架构是主要限制因素。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探究蛋白质构象集合生成模型的设计选择在不同结构蛋白质中的表现，以优化模型性能。

Method: 使用Cα流匹配模型，通过消融实验评估架构、序列条件、刚体对齐和训练数据选择对性能的影响，并与实验数据对比。

Result: 发现训练数据的质量对模型性能影响更大，改进数据源可提升化学位移吻合度，但可能牺牲SAXS一致性。

Conclusion: 训练数据是模型主要限制因素，混合不同数据源可在性能和成本间取得平衡。

Abstract: Generative models of protein conformational ensembles inherit much of their design from structure prediction for folded proteins. Ordered and disordered proteins, however, occupy distinct structural regimes, and we investigate how design choices hold up across them. Using a minimal C$α$ flow-matching model, we ablate choices of architecture, sequence conditioning, rigid-body alignment and training source, and evaluate each variant against experimental SAXS and NMR chemical-shift data partitioned into ordered, disordered, multi-domain and transitional proteins. Mixture-of-experts routing, a static pair representation and a disorder probe have no detectable effect, a smaller protein language model performs worse on ordered proteins but not on disordered ones, and alignment in the loss degrades agreement with SAXS. Replacing coarse-grained simulations of disordered proteins with fragment-assembled ensembles improves agreement with chemical shifts at a cost to SAXS, while an equal mixture of the two keeps most of the gain at a smaller cost. This points to the training data, rather than the architecture, as the main constraint.

*Daniel Aibinder,Azmi Haider,Dan Rosenbaum*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 论文提出了一种名为EB-FM的方法，用于从间接、噪声观测中学习高维潜在变量的生成先验，特别关注冷冻电镜中的连续异质性。该方法通过蒙特卡洛随机近似EM框架直接学习流匹配先验，无需依赖干净数据的预训练。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 冷冻电镜中的3D原子结构无法直接观测，必须从复杂的非线性前向算子生成的2D投影图像中推断。现有方法通常需要干净数据的预训练，且难以处理非线性模型，因此需要一种更直接且适应性强的解决方案。

Method: EB-FM采用蒙特卡洛随机近似EM框架，交替进行E步（通过引导流生成近似后验采样）和M步（利用稳定化的潜在变量更新流模型）。

Result: 在受控逆问题（两月亮和MNIST数据）和模拟冷冻电镜数据上验证了方法的有效性，表明EB-FM可以直接在原子坐标空间中恢复蛋白质构象的连续分布。

Conclusion: EB-FM为处理复杂非线性和间接观测提供了无需预训练的灵活解决方案，在冷冻电镜等领域具有潜在应用价值。

Abstract: We address the problem of learning generative priors over high-dimensional latent variables from indirect, noisy observations, with a focus on continuous heterogeneity in cryo-electron microscopy (cryo-EM). In this setting, 3D atomic structures $x \in \mathbb{R}^{3N_a}$ are never directly observed and must be inferred from 2D projection images $y$ generated by a complex, non-linear forward operator. We propose Empirical Bayes Flow Matching (EB-FM), a method that learns a flow matching prior $p_θ(x)$ directly from observations using a Monte Carlo stochastic approximation Expectation-Maximization framework. EB-FM alternates between an E-step that performs approximate posterior sampling from $p_θ(x \mid y)$ via guided flow-based generation while maintaining persistent latent estimates through stochastic approximation, and an M-step that updates the flow model using these stabilized latent variables as training targets. This approach eliminates the need for pretraining on clean data and naturally accommodates non-linear forward models. We validate our method on controlled inverse problems using two-moons and MNIST data, and demonstrate its effectiveness on simulated cryo-EM data with known poses, showing that EB-FM can recover continuous distributions of protein conformations directly in atomic-coordinate space.

RewardNet smooths the affinity landscape, while TaxNet penalizes reward overexploitation, jointly defining a learned meta-reward for policy optimization.
