### [1] [TopoLIA: Topology-Inspired Latent Interaction Attention for Drug Discovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13006)
*Xiang Liu,Yiming Ren,Mustafa Hajij,Pietro Liò,Guo-Wei Wei*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 论文提出TopoLIA，一种基于拓扑的潜在交互注意力机制，通过紧凑的潜在交互空间和拓扑谱表示改进AI在药物发现中的应用。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 药物发现的复杂性要求AI模型能更好地处理复杂交互，传统Transformer的注意机制成为瓶颈。

Method: TopoLIA结合拓扑谱表示和潜在交互注意力机制，学习信息交换和转换方式。

Result: 在蛋白-配体结合任务中，TopoLIA表现优于传统方法，实现SOTA性能。

Conclusion: TopoLIA为复杂交互提供通用框架，适用于多样化的科学和AI问题。

Abstract: The growing sophistication of drug discovery calls for new advances in artificial intelligence (AI). Transformers have underpinned recent breakthroughs in AI, yet their conventional source-target attention mechanism can become a bottleneck for modeling complex interactions. Here, we introduce TopoLIA, a topology-inspired latent interaction attention mechanism that learns not only how much information should be exchanged between interaction partners, but also how that information should be transformed. Source-target-dependent attention is mediated through a compact latent interaction space, providing a structured representation of complex interactions. We further incorporate topological spectral representations to capture multiscale data geometry and extract algebraic relationships. TopoLIA thus incorporates topological principles into both data representation and neural network architecture. We demonstrate TopoLIA on protein-ligand binding, where persistent spectra encode multiscale topological and geometric patterns in 3D protein-ligand complexes, while latent interaction attention (LIA) enables structured, source-target-dependent information exchange. We pretrain TopoLIA by reconstructing masked multiscale spectral tokens from unlabeled molecular structures, yielding transferable structural representations without task-specific annotations. Across multiple scoring and docking benchmarks, TopoLIA achieves state-of-the-art performance and consistently outperforms its standard-attention counterpart. Rank analyses show that compact latent dimensions yield the best performance, supporting compact interaction spaces as an effective inductive bias for biomolecular learning. More broadly, LIA provides a general framework for structured, source-target-dependent information exchange that is applicable to diverse scientific and AI problems involving complex heterogeneous interactions.

### [2] [MORAD: Reinforcement Learning with Multi-Objective Rewards for RNA Inverse Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13305)
*Jixin Tang,Ji Guo,Jun Zhao*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: MORAD是一个新型在线强化学习框架，用于训练一个通用的RNA扩散模型，优化多目标设计任务。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的RNA逆设计方法要么为每个目标骨架单独优化策略，要么依赖离线偏好优化，因此需要一种能够在线训练并可重用的骨架条件模型。

Method: MORAD结合了骨架条件组采样、组相对奖励归一化和前向扩散目标，同时优化六个不同方向的测量指标。

Result: MORAD在多目标优化中表现出色，显著提升了GDT-TS、配对MCC等11项指标中的10项，测试集上优于先前方法。

Conclusion: MORAD在结构条件RNA逆设计中实现了新的最先进水平。

Abstract: We introduce MORAD, an online reinforcement learning framework for backbone-conditioned RNA diffusion models that trains a single shared policy across diverse target backbones. Structure-conditioned RNA inverse design seeks nucleotide sequences compatible with a target three-dimensional backbone, yet existing reinforcement learning approaches either optimize a separate policy for each target backbone or rely on offline preference optimization, leaving online post-training of a reusable backbone-conditioned model largely unexplored. MORAD combines backbone-conditioned group sampling, group-relative reward normalization, and a forward-process diffusion objective. Beyond existing RL rewards that primarily target tertiary structural quality, MORAD jointly optimizes six measurements spanning tertiary geometry, base pairing, ensemble behavior, and sequence composition. To aggregate these heterogeneous objectives despite differences in scale and optimization direction, MORAD maps each measurement to a bounded desirability score and combines them with a weighted geometric mean. Compared with a tertiary-structure-only reward, this multi-objective formulation improves 10 of the 11 evaluation metrics that have a preferred direction. Applied to pretrained RIDE, MORAD improves GDT-TS by 16.0% (from 0.3300 to 0.3827) and pairing MCC by 19.4% (from 0.6110 to 0.7295), while reducing normalized ensemble defect by 27.5% (from 0.3906 to 0.2830). On the test set, MORAD outperforms RiboDiffusion, gRNAde, and RDesign across tertiary structural accuracy, secondary-structure pairing, and thermodynamic quality, establishing a new state of the art in structure-conditioned RNA inverse design.

### [3] [Designing Protein Structures from Natural Language](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13917)
*Joseph Lee,Yanjing Li,Bo Qiang,Gregory Lau,Allyson Ettinger,Noah A. Smith,Rory Donovan-Maiye,David Baker,Hanna Hajishirzi,Pang Wei Koh*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: Prompteina是一个基于自然语言的蛋白质设计模型，通过文本描述生成蛋白质结构，支持更广泛的设计目标，并在多个指标上优于现有方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有蛋白质设计模型通常需要针对每种属性设计专门的输入表示，限制了设计目标的范围。本研究旨在开发一种通过自然语言描述直接生成蛋白质结构的模型。

Method: 利用Prompteina-Data（包含180万蛋白质结构和5000万+文本描述的数据集），对预训练模型Proteina-Complexa进行文本条件预训练和微调，使其能够根据文本描述生成蛋白质结构。

Result: Prompteina在18种结构属性中的15种上优于基线模型，生成蛋白质更符合功能描述；在20个靶标中的13个上表现出色，界面置信度提高14%；通过迭代反馈，其增益比其他基线高57%。实验验证了9个针对GM2A的结合体，最强结合体的KD值为107 nM。

Conclusion: 自然语言作为蛋白质结构生成的调节接口是有效且强大的，Prompteina为蛋白质设计开辟了新途径。

Abstract: A central challenge in de novo protein design is generating proteins with desired structural and functional properties. Existing design models typically require a dedicated input representation for each property, limiting the design objectives they support. We develop Prompteina, the first all-atom structure generation model that supports new and broader design objectives specified in natural language, analogous to text-to-image generation. To train Prompteina, we construct Prompteina-Data, a paired text--protein structure dataset of 1.8M structures and 50M+ captions describing global and residue-specific structural and functional properties. We adapt Proteina-Complexa, a pretrained structure generation model, through text-conditioned pretraining and finetuning that aligns residues with their corresponding text. Prompteina outperforms baselines on 15 of 18 structural properties and generates proteins that more closely match descriptions of their intended functions. In binder design, it leads baselines on at least 13 of 20 targets and improves interface confidence by 14\% over the strongest baseline. With iterative in silico feedback, Prompteina's gain over non-iterative generation is 57\% larger than the next-best baseline on the hardest targets. Finally, we experimentally validate Prompteina designs against GM2A, a target with no reported de novo binders. We identify nine binders, with the strongest at an apparent $K_{\mathrm{D}}$ of 107 nM. These results establish natural language as an effective and powerful conditioning interface for protein structure generation.
