### [1] [Unlocking the Full Potential of Taylor Dispersion Analysis Through Advanced Detection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30039)
*Rami Laoufi,Agnès Hagège*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 摘要综述了泰勒分散分析（TDA）在制药科学中的应用，强调其作为多功能工具在测量药物载体大小方面的价值。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 药物载体的尺寸测量对确保药物安全性、稳定性和有效性至关重要，而TDA因其适用范围广和可耦合多种检测器而成为重要方法。

Method: 通过综述TDA在不同应用场景中的研究（如尺寸测量、聚集研究、相互作用研究等），探讨如何根据应用需求选择检测器。

Result: TDA被证明是一种高度通用的工具，适用于从简单缓冲液到复杂培养基（如血清）的多种分析。

Conclusion: TDA因其多功能性和适应性，在制药科学中展现出广阔的应用前景。

Abstract: Today, pharmaceutical sciences rely on a wide range of innovative therapeutics of varying sizes: nanoparticles, proteins, antibodies, and nanocarriers for drug delivery. Measuring their size is one of the crucial parameters in their characterization, to ensure drug safety, stability, and effectiveness. Over the past two decades, Taylor Dispersion Analysis has emerged as an important sizing method. Its scope of application is perfectly suited to the sizes of these therapeutics. By design, it can be coupled with a wide range of detectors, and extensive research has been conducted in this field. In this review, we will present an overview of the studies conducted, and discuss how the application drives the choice of one detector or another (size measurement, aggregation studies, interaction studies, and other analyses, performed in both simple biological buffers and in more complex media, particularly serum) and makes Taylor Dispersion Analysis a highly versatile tool.

### [2] [QUBO-Based Conformer Selection with Quantum-Classical Hybrid Optimization for Multiconformer Ligand Modeling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29058)
*Jiyun Lee,Haeseong Kim,Junho Lee,Joonsuk Huh*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 该研究将配体构象选择问题重新形式化为QUBO问题，并使用量子-经典混合求解器解决，结果显示其优于传统MIQP方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探索更高效的配体多构象建模方法，以提升构象选择的准确性和效率。

Method: 将构象选择问题转化为QUBO问题，使用量子-经典混合求解器，并与传统MIQP方法对比。

Result: 在83个X射线配体条目中，量子-qFit在74.7%的条目中表现出更高的RSCC，79.5%的条目中表现出更低的扭曲应变。

Conclusion: QUBO框架为多构象配体选择提供了新的研究途径，且优于传统方法。

Abstract: A ligand can adopt multiple conformations within the same binding site, and these conformations can interact differently with the surrounding protein. Multiconformer modeling must therefore determine which candidate conformations to retain and what occupancies to assign to them. An established automated approach uses mixed-integer quadratic programming (MIQP) with hard occupancy and conformer-count limits for this selection. Here, we reformulated conformer selection as a quadratic unconstrained binary optimization (QUBO) problem with finite penalties and discrete occupancies. We solved the QUBO using a quantum-classical hybrid solver. We compared this workflow with the MIQP-based qFit-ligand workflow across 83 paired X-ray ligand entries using the same experimental inputs and the same qFit-ligand processing protocol. For each entry, both final models were evaluated against the same composite-omit map generated from the common input structure before the two workflows diverged. Quantum-qFit produced higher ligand-region real-space correlation coefficients (RSCC) in 62 entries 74.7% and lower occupancy-weighted torsional strain in 66 entries 79.5%. Similar directional trends remained in the 28 entries with matched final conformer counts. The molecular Electron Density for Individual Atoms score (EDIAm) decreased in most entries. This result shows that higher regional map agreement did not necessarily correspond to stronger atom-level density support. Overall, the results show that the conformer-selection formulation can change which ligand states are represented from the same experimental data and provide a QUBO framework for studying multiconformer ligand selection.

</details>
