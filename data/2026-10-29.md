### [1] [Accurate structural modelling of protein-glycan recognition with GlycoWeave](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37410)
*Huifeng Zhao,Jintu Zhang,Yifei Xiao,Youqiao Qian,Silong Zhai,Zichang Jin,Li Wang,Yuchao Wang,Hang Dong,Yifan Zhao,Zhenxing Wu,Hongyan Du,Yuansheng Huang,Bowei Zhao,Xiaorui Wang,Shukai Gu,Jike Wang,Zhongjun Li,Zhongtang Li,Tingjun Hou,Yu Kang*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: GlycoWeave是一个用于预测蛋白质-糖复合物结构的扩散框架，结合化学约束采样和多源学习，效果优于AlphaFold 3，且高效且化学准确。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 蛋白质-糖识别在生物学中具有重要意义，但由于糖类的化学多样性和结构灵活性，预测其复合物结构仍具挑战。

Method: GlycoWeave采用内坐标空间扩散框架，结合多尺度分子表示和多源学习，保持糖类连接性和立体化学。

Result: 在698个蛋白质-糖复合物测试中，GlycoWeave的高质量预测成功率为71.9%，远超AlphaFold 3的19.2%，且速度更快。

Conclusion: GlycoWeave是一种高效、准确且化学可靠的蛋白质-糖识别建模工具。

Abstract: Protein-glycan recognition underlies diverse biological processes, yet accurate prediction of protein-glycan complex structures remains challenging owing to the chemical diversity, conformational flexibility and sparse experimental structural coverage of glycans. Here we present GlycoWeave, a glycan-specific diffusion framework that integrates chemically constrained conformational sampling, multiscale molecular representations and multi-source learning. By operating in internal-coordinate space, GlycoWeave preserves glycan connectivity and stereochemistry while efficiently exploring bound conformations. A heterogeneous equivariant network couples atom-level interaction geometry with monosaccharide-level structural context, whereas multi-source, multi-head training integrates experimental complexes with structures refined using classical and quantum mechanics-informed potentials. On a benchmark of 698 protein-glycan complexes, GlycoWeave achieves a 71.9% Top-25 high-quality success rate, compared with 19.2% for AlphaFold 3, while requiring only 0.34 s per sampled pose. GlycoWeave further preserves glycan structural validity, recovers native protein-glycan interactions and reproduces linkage-specific conformational preferences, maintaining high accuracy across diverse glycan architectures and protein environments. These results establish GlycoWeave as an accurate, efficient and chemically faithful framework for modelling protein-glycan recognition. GlycoWeave is freely available at https://cadd.zju.edu.cn/glycoweave.

### [2] [A unified coarse-grained neural network potential for the dynamics of biomolecular complexes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37606)
*Antonio Mirarchi,Stephen E. Farr,Adrià Peréz,Gianni De Fabritiis*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: AMARO v2.0是一种粗粒化的神经网络势，能够隐式处理溶剂并保留所有重原子。它适用于蛋白质、药物样分子及其复合物的模拟，能够区分蛋白质构象自由能景观的关键特征，并在固定GPU时间预算下优于全原子分子力学。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 为了解决原子分子动力学难以捕捉生物分子复合物中罕见事件的问题，研究提出了AMARO v2.0。

Method: AMARO v2.0使用氢感知的珠子类型和总电荷条件，适用于蛋白质-蛋白质-配体三元系统，并保留了蛋白质的折叠状态。

Result: 在262个复合物的测试中，模型能够保留大多数配体姿态，但在HIF2A三元复合物中观察到四级结构重定向。在特定案例中，AMARO v2.0表现优于全原子分子力学。

Conclusion: AMARO v2.0作为一种粗粒化势，展示了在生物分子复合物动力学中的潜力，但仍存在结合姿态恢复和位点特异性等局限性。

Abstract: The behavior of biomolecular complexes is governed by chemically specific interactions and often involves rare events that atomistic molecular dynamics struggle to capture within accessible timescales. Here we introduce AMARO v2.0, a coarse-grained neural network potential that retains all heavy atoms and treats solvent implicitly. Hydrogen-aware bead types and total-charge conditioning provide a shared representation for proteins, drug-like molecules, and their complexes, including ternary protein-protein-ligand systems. The model preserves folded proteins and captures key features of protein conformational free-energy landscapes. Across 262 complexes spanning 11 targets, the learned potential retains most ligand poses, although quaternary structure reorientation is observed for the HIF2A ternary complex. In targeted case studies, AMARO v2.0 distinguishes the two protonation states of trypsin-benzamidine and reaches the crystal binding pose in 3.3-fold more trajectories than all-atom molecular mechanics at a fixed estimated GPU-time budget. The same framework also recovers a reference binding pose in TYK2 and supports binding free-energy estimation for both trypsin-benzamidine and TYK2-EJM31, although the resulting values are more energetically favorable than the experimental references. SHP2 and CDK8 systems expose remaining limitations in binding-pose recovery and site specificity, respectively. Together, these results support AMARO v2.0 as a shared heavy-atom potential for biomolecular complex dynamics.
