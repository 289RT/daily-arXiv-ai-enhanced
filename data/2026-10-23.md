### [1] [ProMORNA: An End-to-End Generative Framework for Full-Length mRNA Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01513)
*Zixi Shao,Tao Wang,Ye Shen,Tianyi Huang,Yibei Xiao*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: ProMORNA是一种零射击、端到端的生成框架，用于设计全长mRNA，通过两阶段后训练过程平衡稳定性和翻译效率，在实验中表现优于现有方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有mRNA设计方法依赖分区生成或耗时搜索，ProMORNA旨在克服这些限制，提供更高效的全长mRNA生成方案。

Method: 采用BART架构预训练，结合冷启动监督微调和多目标强化学习的后训练阶段，直接优化生成策略以适应生物约束。

Result: 在计算机评估和体外实验中，ProMORNA在稳定性和翻译效率上超越基准方法和GEMORNA，表现更优。

Conclusion: ProMORNA是生成功能性全长mRNA的有前途框架，实验结果验证了其有效性。

Abstract: Designing therapeutic messenger RNA (mRNA) requires generating full-length transcripts that optimally balance intracellular stability and translation efficiency. While generative models have advanced mRNA design, existing approaches rely on region-wise generation, expensive inference-time search, or target-specific model updates. To overcome these limitations, we introduce ProMORNA, a zero-shot, end-to-end generative framework for full-length mRNA design. ProMORNA aligns the design process with modern Large Language Model training pipelines: it utilizes a BART-style architecture pre-trained on over 6 million natural protein-mRNA pairs, followed by a two-stage post-training process comprising cold-start supervised fine-tuning and multi-objective reinforcement learning. By standardizing and aggregating relative advantages at the individual metric level with domain-specific stabilization, our post-training stage directly aligns the generative policy with conflicting biological constraints while avoiding direct scalarization of raw objective scores. In silico evaluations demonstrate that ProMORNA pushes the Pareto frontier of predicted stability and translation efficiency beyond standard baselines and GEMORNA, a leading state-of-the-art method. In three independent in vitro Fluc2P experiments, Candidate 2 achieved a 12-72h reporter-activity AUC 3.33 times that of the conventional Benchmark (Holm-adjusted $P=0.01285$), while its estimated 1.29-fold advantage over the reported best GEMORNA candidate remained exploratory. These results support ProMORNA as a promising framework for generating functionally active full-length mRNA candidates.

### [2] [AI Mindmeld: A Latent Interconnect Protocol](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30215)
*Siavash Golkar,Jake Kovalic,Shirley Ho*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种名为LIP的协议，通过直接表示交换解决LLMs与专家模型间的信息传输瓶颈，展示了其在科学代理中的应用潜力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: LLMs在作为科学代理时，需要与专家模型交换信息，但传统的文本、图像等传输方式成为瓶颈，限制了信息的丰富性和维度。

Method: LIP协议通过学习连接器将专家模型的表示映射到LLMs的内部状态，仅训练连接器，避免了修改预训练模型。

Result: 实验表明，LIP能有效传递信息支持新任务，且任务性能和组合性随LLMs能力提升而改善，尤其在零样本任务迁移中表现突出。

Conclusion: 尽管当前LLMs尚未针对专家表示进行优化，但通过改进表示接口和LLMs的训练，可进一步扩展科学代理的能力。

Abstract: LLMs can act as scientific agents using tools to consult specialists and run experiments. Yet specialist information must enter their context, generally as text, images, audio or video: a bottleneck between models with rich, high-dimensional representations. We advocate direct representation exchange: a mindmeld conveying many properties, including information an LLM would not think to request. It should be extensible to new specialists, flexible across questions and tasks, and compositional across independently connected specialists without joint training.
  The Latent Interconnect Protocol (LIP) learns connectors mapping specialist representations into LLM internal states. Training only connectors keeps this practical as LLMs grow and evolve. We connect three protein and three small molecule specialists to Gemma 3 (4B and 12B parameters) and Kimi K2.6 (1T total, 32B active), freezing all pretrained models. Independently trained connectors provide useful information without changing the LLM or existing connectors, supporting familiar and new question types about unseen objects. We demonstrate zero-shot transfer to cross-domain tasks without joint training. Task performance and compositionality generally improve with LLM capability, with especially strong gains in zero-shot task transfer. Limitations remain: current LLMs were not trained for specialist representations as native inputs; access does not ensure reliable interpretation, integration or use in reasoning. We call on specialist developers to provide consistent representation interfaces and, especially, on LLM developers to train models to receive, combine and reason with these inputs. These efforts could expand scientific agents' information while preserving conventional tools' flexibility.
  Data, pretrained connectors, and code for training, evaluation and interactive inference: https://github.com/PolymathicAI/AI-Mindmeld

### [3] [Escaping the Phylogenetic Trap: Persistent Homology Enables Cross-Species Ion Channel Classification via Multimodal Graph Neural Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30394)
*Arezoo Bybordi,Favour Achimba,Mariam Gelashvili,Jessy Ramirez,Weigang Qiu,Mandë Holford,Anita Raja*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 蛋白分类器性能受评估协议影响显著，随机拆分会导致系统发育泄漏，导致模型高估泛化能力。本文提出了一种基于拓扑表示的跨物种预测方法，证明其在分类学偏移下优于其他方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有蛋白分类器在随机拆分评估中表现良好，但在分类学偏移下泛化能力不足，尤其是毒液化合物分类任务中。需要一种能捕捉跨物种不变特征的表示方法。

Method: 采用多模态框架结合ESM-2序列嵌入和基于拓扑表示的图注意力网络，对比五种结构先验在留一分类学组评估下的表现。

Result: 随机拆分下所有模型表现相似（81-86%），但分类学偏移时，拓扑表示（PH-GNN）以63.4%准确率显著优于其他方法（54-55%）。

Conclusion: 随机拆分评估掩盖了表示质量的差异，拓扑表示是处理生物分布偏移的有效先验。

Abstract: Protein classifiers are highly sensitive to evaluation protocol: random train/test splits induce phylogenetic leakage, allowing models to exploit taxon-specific correlations and substantially overestimate out-of-distribution performance. This issue is particularly acute for venom compound classification by ion-channel target (Ca2+, K+, Na+), where reported accuracies of 80-90% do not reflect generalization to unseen taxa. We formulate cross-species prediction as a structured distribution shift in which taxonomic identity acts as a confounder, requiring representations that capture structural features invariant across phylogeny. Using a shared multimodal backbone that fuses ESM-2 sequence embeddings with a disulfide-aware graph attention network, we systematically compare five structural inductive biases (spectral, Riemannian, SE(3)-invariant, statistical-manifold, and persistent-homology representations) under a leave-one-taxon-out evaluation protocol on a benchmark of 1,373 ColabFold-predicted venom compounds spanning five taxa. The persistent-homology representation summarizes backbone geometry through topological descriptors that are robust to local structural perturbations, providing an inductive bias toward phylogenetically invariant learning. Under conventional i.i.d. splits, all models achieve similar accuracy (81-86%), masking differences in inductive bias. Under taxonomic shift, this parity collapses: a sequence-only baseline drops to approximately 30%, the remaining geometric priors cluster at 54-55%, while our persistent-homology model (PH-GNN) achieves 63.4%, outperforming the next-best method by over 8 percentage points. These results demonstrate that random-split evaluation obscures meaningful differences in representation quality and identify topological representations as an effective inductive bias for learning under structured biological distribution shift.

### [4] [RiboBridge: protein-conditioned de novo RNA binder structure design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30668)
*Zhou Zhang,Hanqun Cao,Minghao Sun,Odin Zhang,Yang Zhang,Pheng Ann Heng,Wujun Li,Tianfan Fu*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: RiboBridge是一种新的结构优先框架，通过分离RNA构象生成和蛋白质-RNA组装过程，利用交叉框架相互作用几何学设计RNA分子，显著提升了复合物恢复的准确性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有方法通常在一个统一表示中耦合RNA构象生成和蛋白质-RNA组装，导致两者纠缠不清。RiboBridge旨在通过分离这两个过程，提高设计的灵活性和准确性。

Method: RiboBridge首先在蛋白质表面提出多个候选RNA结合界面，然后在预训练的RNA结构潜在空间（RiboSphere）中生成规范RNA构象，并通过预测蛋白质-RNA相互作用约束将独立的RNA和蛋白质坐标框架桥接起来。最终通过刚性放置将相互作用几何转换为几何一致的蛋白质相对姿态。

Result: 在124个蛋白质-RNA复合物的基准测试中，RiboBridge在fNAT、DockQ、界面RMSD和配体RMSD等指标上表现最佳。组件消融和阶段诊断进一步揭示了复合物恢复误差的主要来源。

Conclusion: RiboBridge通过分离RNA构象生成和蛋白质-RNA组装过程，显著提升了复合物设计的准确性，为未来RNA-蛋白质相互作用研究提供了新方法。

Abstract: Designing RNA molecules that bind to target protein structures requires generating both an RNA conformation and its bound complex geometry, without assuming a predefined binding site. Existing approaches generally model these coupled processes within a unified representation, entangling RNA conformation generation with protein-relative placement. In this work, we introduce RiboBridge, a structure-first framework that separates RNA conformational generation from protein--RNA assembly through cross-frame interaction geometry. RiboBridge first proposes multiple candidate RNA-binding interfaces on the protein surface, generates canonical RNA conformations within a pretrained RNA structural latent space (RiboSphere), and then predicts protein--RNA interaction constraints to bridge the independently defined RNA and protein coordinate frames. The resulting interaction geometry is converted into a geometrically consistent protein-relative pose through rigid placement. We evaluate RiboBridge on a temporally split, sequence-filtered, and redundancy-controlled benchmark of 124 protein--RNA complexes. Under post-hoc interface-correspondence evaluation, where native information is used only after generation to select among already generated predicted-pocket candidates, RiboBridge achieves the highest fNAT and DockQ and the lowest interface and ligand RMSDs among compared methods. Component ablations and stage-wise diagnostics further characterize the contributions of RNA conformational generation, interaction prediction, and rigid placement, identifying the main sources of complex-recovery error.

</details>

### [5] [Supercoiling Drives DNA-Protein Co-Condensation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30588)
*Sihan Liu,Hongbo Zhao,Andrej Košmrlj*

Main category: physics.bio-ph

TL;DR: 论文研究了DNA和蛋白质的共缩合过程中DNA超螺旋对其的影响，揭示了超螺旋通过促进蛋白湿润和DNA构象变化，驱动共缩合的机制。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 理解DNA超螺旋如何在基因组压缩中调节蛋白质和DNA的共缩合过程。

Method: 通过将DNA弹性与蛋白质浓度场耦合，模拟超螺旋对共缩合的作用。

Result: 超螺旋通过促进蛋白质湿润和DNA构象变化，移除成核屏障，实现自发共缩合。

Conclusion: 拓扑结构是生物分子缩合的新调控参数，为基因组组织的动态调控提供了机械框架。

Abstract: The co-condensation of DNA and proteins plays an important role in genome compaction, including heterochromatin formation and chromosome segregation. Meanwhile, transcription and replication continuously generate DNA supercoiling, yet how the resulting torsional stress affects co-condensation remains poorly understood. Here, by coupling DNA elasticity to a protein concentration field, we show that supercoiling acts as a topological catalyst for co-condensation through a mutually reinforcing interplay with protein wetting. Specifically, protein wetting facilitates twist-to-writhe conversion, generating highly writhed DNA conformations that in turn promote capillary collapse into a protein-rich droplet. In the absence of supercoiling, DNA remains kinetically trapped in metastable extended states within our model, whereas sufficiently strong supercoiling removes the nucleation barrier and enables spontaneous co-condensation. These results establish topology as a distinct control parameter for biomolecular condensation and provide a mechanical framework for how active genomic processes can dynamically regulate large-scale genome organization.

</details>
