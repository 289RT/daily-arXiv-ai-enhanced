### [1] [A Description-Derived Drug-Drug Interaction Benchmark Reveals Split-Dependent Molecular Representation Rankings](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32371)
*Manel Gil-Sorribes,Isaac Filella-Merce,Alexis Molina*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 该论文研究了药物-药物相互作用（DDI）预测的分子表示学习方法，通过构建文本衍生类别数据集并比较不同分子表示方法的表现。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: DDI预测在临床上具有重要意义，但与单一分子性质预测不同，它需要将两种分子表示组合成关系结果。现有的DrugBank数据提供了文本描述而非分类标签，因此需要从文本中提取分类目标。

Method: 研究者构建了一个包含六种类别的DDI数据集，定义了四种不同的训练测试分割方式（从随机分割到支架不相交评估），并比较了分子指纹、预训练序列表示和图衍生特征在不同分割方式下的表现。

Result: 实验结果表明，分子指纹在高重叠随机分割中表现最佳，而图衍生表示在药物和支架不相交分割中表现更好，且随着分子重叠限制的增加，其优势更加明显；分类器头的变化不影响分子表示的相对排名。

Conclusion: 研究发现，分子表示的表现排名会随着测试集新颖性的变化而变化，这为DDI预测方法的选择提供了指导。

Abstract: Drug-drug interaction (DDI) prediction is clinically important and poses a distinct challenge for molecular representation learning. Unlike single-molecule property prediction, DDI requires a predictor to compose two molecular representations into a relational outcome. DrugBank provides curator-authored DDI descriptions rather than ready-made categorical labels, so a categorical prediction target must first be derived from the text. We construct a dataset of DDI pairs by mapping processed descriptions to six text-derived categories and define a benchmark with four holdout regimes that restrict train-test chemical overlap from random pairs to scaffold-disjoint evaluation. We then use this benchmark to compare molecular fingerprints, pretrained sequence representations, and graph-derived features across five seeds and multiple classifier heads. Molecular fingerprints lead the high- overlap random split, whereas graph-derived representations lead the drug- and scaffold-holdout regimes, with the gap increasing as molecular overlap is restricted. Changing the classifier head affects absolute performance but not the relative representation ranking. These results show that representation rankings change with test-set novelty under a fixed description-derived label space.

### [2] [Evolutionary constraints on protein fold diversity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33104)
*Harutyun Sahakyan,Eugene V. Koonin*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 论文探讨了天然蛋白质折叠多样性有限的原因，研究了生物物理约束和进化偶然性的影响。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究天然蛋白质折叠多样性有限的原因是生物物理约束还是进化偶然性。

Method: 结合蛋白质结构预测和比较、分子设计以及冷冻电镜技术的新方法。

Result: 发现天然蛋白质仅采用了所有可能折叠中的一小部分。

Conclusion: 进化过程限制了蛋白质折叠的多样性。

Abstract: Arrangements of secondary structures in proteins yield a wide variety of possible topologies, yet the protein folds observed in nature are limited in their diversity. Is the number of protein folds limited by biophysical constraints or by evolutionary contingency? In recent years, the development of new methods for protein structure prediction and comparison, molecular design, and the rapid progress of cryo-electron microscopy revealed new insights into the protein fold space. Here, we present recent findings demonstrating that natural proteins adopt only a small fraction of all possible folds and discuss how evolutionary processes limit the diversity of evolving protein folds.

### [3] [Beyond Hard Tokens: Predictive Memory for Multimodal Protein Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32347)
*Xiaoliang Shi,Zichen Wang,Zhijian Wei,Zhanpeng Shi,Zhenghong Zhou,Yunpeng Wang,Shuangjia Zheng*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: PredMem是一种无训练的自调节方法，通过跨步骤的软预测稳定多模态蛋白质语言模型的联合设计。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 解决多模态蛋白质语言模型在联合设计时因缺乏稳定跨步骤软锚而导致的收敛慢或退化问题。

Method: 引入PredMem，利用概率加权的Top-k期望嵌入在每一步传递未承诺的软预测，以稳定模型生成。

Result: 在多个模型中，PredMem显著提高了序列-结构联合生成的成功率和质量多样性，抑制了重复崩溃。

Conclusion: PredMem通过早期轨迹稳定和多模态协同进化，有效提升了离散多模态生成的效率和效果。

Abstract: Multimodal protein language models unify sequence and discrete structural tokens via iterative masked decoding, offering a promising paradigm for joint protein design. However, standard decoders rely on a memoryless mask reset at each iteration: only a sparse subset of newly committed hard tokens is carried forward, while all remaining unresolved sites are reset to an identical [MASK] embedding. In open-ended co-generation where neither modality provides a fixed external anchor, this stateless transition limits single-step refinement depth and deprives both tracks of stable cross-step soft anchors, leading to slow convergence or degenerative repetitive collapse. Observing that uncommitted predictions evolve with pronounced temporal coherence rather than erratic noise, we introduce Predictive Memory (PredMem), a training-free self-conditioning recurrence that carries these soft beliefs across steps. At each step, PredMem summarizes raw logits into a probability-weighted Top-$k$ expectation embedding at every residue and affinely interpolates it into eligible masked inputs on the next step at zero extra denoiser cost. Across DPLM-2, DPLM-2.1, and ESM3, PredMem raises unconditional sequence-structure co-generation success by 3.6, 17.7, and 48.7 percentage points, respectively, while improving the quality-diversity trade-off and suppressing repetitive mode collapse. Controlled diagnostics show that these gains stem primarily from early trajectory stabilization in unanchored cross-modal co-evolution rather than extra compute or simple entropy reduction, establishing PredMem as an efficient inference mechanism for multimodal discrete generation.

</details>
