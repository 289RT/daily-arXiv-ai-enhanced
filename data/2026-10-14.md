### [1] [Calibrating AlphaFold confidence scores for interface quality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17108)
*Sagyndyk Sagadinov,Vsevolod Viliuga,Arne Elofsson*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: AlphaFold置信度分数被广泛用于蛋白质复合物预测的排名和模型选择，但其数值并非设计为表示预测满足结构质量标准的概率。本研究通过校准这些分数，提高了其与DockQ标准的可靠对应性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: AlphaFold的置信度分数虽然能有效区分预测质量，但并未直接表示预测满足特定结构质量标准的概率。作者希望将这些分数校准为更可靠的DockQ通过概率。

Method: 作者采用后验等张校准方法，将常用置信度分数转换为满足DockQ阈值的概率，并在抗体-抗原和一般多聚体数据集上验证其效果。

Result: 校准后的分数显著提高了预测概率与实际DockQ通过率的一致性，同时保持了原始分数的排序。即使在少量目标域样本的情况下，也能恢复可靠估计。

Conclusion: 这种无需微调AlphaFold模型的后验校准方法，提供了一种简单且可定制的方式，将模型质量排名转换为满足结构质量标准的校准概率。

Abstract: AlphaFold confidence scores are widely used to rank protein complex predictions and to select subsets of models for downstream applications. Although these scores provide strong discriminatory power for distinguishing prediction quality, their numerical values were never intended to represent the probability that a prediction satisfies a given structural quality criterion. In this work, we calibrate commonly used confidence scores to estimate the probability that a predicted complex exceeds a chosen threshold of DockQ, an established measure of interface quality. On antibody-antigen and general multimer datasets, post-hoc isotonic calibration consistently improves agreement between predicted probabilities and observed DockQ success rates while preserving the underlying score ordering. The calibrated probabilities also provide more accurate estimates of the fraction of selected models expected to pass a given structural quality threshold. Although the mapping is fitted within a given prediction distribution and therefore transfers imperfectly across prediction domains, we show that reliable estimates are recovered from a small number of labeled target-domain samples. Because the calibration is entirely post hoc and requires no underlying AlphaFold model fine-tuning, it provides a straightforward and customizable way to convert existing model quality rankings into calibrated probabilities of passing a chosen structural quality threshold.
