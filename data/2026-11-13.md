### [1] [Silscore: modelling siRNA potency with positional thermodynamics and data-selected nucleotide-identity terms](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15812)
*Giuseppe Sacco,Isabella Baldini,Riccardo Paone,Mattia Capulli,Guido Sanguinetti,Giovanni Bussi*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: Silscore是一个基于小分子干扰RNA（siRNA）的基因沉默预测模型，结合热力学和稀疏核苷酸身份特征，具有高预测性能和鲁棒性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的siRNA预测模型在准确性或适应性上存在不足，需要一种更紧凑且性能优异的模型。

Method: Silscore通过整体双链稳定性、近邻堆叠能量的位置解析以及稀疏核苷酸身份项构建，仅用26个参数。

Result: 在多个数据集上，Silscore表现出最高Pearson相关性，并在转移测试中优于其他模型。

Conclusion: Silscore结合了强预测能力和新场景下的鲁棒性，且参数可解释性高。

Abstract: Small interfering RNAs (siRNAs) enable programmable gene silencing, but candidates targeting the same transcript can differ markedly in potency. Existing predictors range from interpretable empirical rules with limited accuracy to high-capacity models that can lose performance when datasets or targets change. We present Silscore, a guide-only model that combines a quadratic preference for overall duplex stability, a position-resolved profile of nearest-neighbour stacking energies and sparse nucleotide-identity terms selected from residuals left unexplained by thermodynamics. Its main fit uses 26 parameters, fewer than half as many as any trainable comparator evaluated here. We evaluate Silscore on the canonical split of the Huesken dataset, two external datasets (Mixset and Katoh-Suzuki), and an evaluation in which the same guides are tested after training on dissimilar targets. Silscore attains the highest observed Pearson correlation in all three transfer evaluations, and the data support an advantage over every applicable comparator on Katoh-Suzuki. For a fixed evaluation set, we compare two equally sized training sets with higher or lower sequence similarity to the evaluation sequences. After training on the less similar sequences, Silscore has the highest observed correlation, and the data do not establish a change in its performance. The fitted parameters can also be interpreted directly: identity terms locate sequence effects that base-pairing energies cannot express, global stability has a data-estimated intermediate optimum, and local energy profiles recover terminal asymmetry and a preference for weaker seed and stronger central pairing. Silscore thus provides a compact, data-driven model that combines strong predictive performance with robustness when tested in new settings and expresses previously reported empirical preferences as quantities estimated from data.

### [2] [CupOFMoCA: Coupled Objective-Guided Discrete Flows for Molecular Conjugate Assembly](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16099)
*Ruoxi Rosie Zhang,Ziang Li,Jiatao Gu,Pranam Chatterjee*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: CupOFMoCA通过耦合生成方法解决了分子共轭物设计中的化学约束问题，显著提高了有效性和效率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统方法独立设计分子共轭物的各组分，忽略了跨组分依赖性，导致组装后化学无效或功能丧失。

Method: 提出CupOFMoCA，一种离散生成方法，通过化学约束和分子设计目标重新加权局部过渡，实现耦合生成。

Result: 在PDC和PROTAC系统中，CupOFMoCA实现了100%的共轭有效性，并在生成速率和有效性上超越基线。

Conclusion: CupOFMoCA是一种高效的分子共轭物生成工具，支持通过协调组装和分子特性进行药物治疗开发。

Abstract: Molecular conjugates, including PROTACs and peptide-drug conjugates (PDCs), derive their function from the joint behavior of multiple coupled components, yet most generative approaches design these components independently and combine them only after generation. Such staged pipelines ignore cross-component dependencies and often produce conjugates that are chemically invalid, fall outside empirical conjugate distributions, or lose function upon assembly. In this work, we introduce Coupled Objective-Guided Discrete Flows for Molecular Conjugate Assembly (CupOFMoCA), a discrete generative method that enforces cross-component chemical constraints during conjugate generation. CupOFMoCA restricts guided updates to chemically feasible conjugate states and reweights local transitions using molecular design objectives. Across three PDC systems, coupled generation achieved 100% conjugation validity among valid peptides and increased valid yield over matched post-hoc filtering, with comparable predicted binding scores across the full valid sets. In three-seed evaluations of three PROTAC systems, CupOFMoCA achieved the highest assembly validity on two systems and generated more assembly-valid molecules per second than the evaluated baselines. We further evaluated the generated PROTACs with post-hoc DegradeMaster scoring and explicit-solvent molecular dynamics, supporting their predicted activity and conformational properties. Together, these results establish CupOFMoCA as an efficient generator of molecular conjugates, supporting therapeutic development via coordinated control of assembly and molecular properties.

Abstract: Artificial intelligence (AI) is changing how biological research is done. Yet it is often unclear when these models' outputs can be trusted. We frame this as a model's knowledge boundary: the range of inputs, tasks, and conditions over which its outputs are reliable. This review explains how foundation models are built and proposes that five design elements (data, architecture, training scheme, evaluation scheme, and post-analysis) define this boundary. We examine evidence on where current models succeed or fail and present standardized descriptions of model limits so that researchers, and the agents acting for them, can use these tools reliably.

### [4] [Using an energy pixels approach to estimate the size a 2nd Generation DEL must be to train a high-resolution binding model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17121)
*Boris P. Belotserkovskii,R. Edward Watts*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 论文探讨了如何通过第二代DNA编码库（DEL）改进机器学习模型，以更有效地指导药物化学优化，提出了一种无结构信息的方法估算所需库的复杂性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统DEL虽然能发现小分子结合剂，但缺乏密集采样和足够的高亲和力结合剂，导致机器学习模型难以准确预测化学修饰对亲和力的影响。

Method: 通过将结合视为多个独立小相互作用（"像素"与"反像素"）的叠加，从初始DEL数据中估计第二代DEL所需的复杂性，无需合成或结构信息。

Result: 该方法能够将模型所需的高亲和力结合剂数量转化为库必须包含的化合物数量，并为随机设计的第二代DEL提供了预期产出的基线。

Conclusion: 提出的方法为优化DEL设计提供了低成本的理论基准，可用于评估机器学习或主动学习在库设计中的增益效果。

Abstract: DNA-encoded libraries (DELs) are commonly used to find small-molecule binders to a target protein. But the hard part is no longer finding a hit. It is building a machine-learning model good enough to guide the medicinal chemistry that follows, one that can resolve the shift in affinity when a chemist changes a single atom. Such a model needs two things from its training data: dense sampling of the small patch of chemical space around the hit, and an adequate number of genuinely tight binders within it. A diverse naive DEL gives neither. It samples an enormous space, so any one neighborhood is covered far too thinly, and the tightest binders are rare. A model trained on that naive DEL data must extrapolate to binders tighter than any it has seen, and that is where models can fail. One way to get training data with both properties is a second-generation DEL built around the hit. We ask how complex that library must be. We treat binding as a sum of small, independent interactions, called pixels on the target and antipixels on the ligand. From the spread of affinities measured in the naive library, with no structural information and nothing yet synthesized, we estimate the complexity such a library must have to supply that dense sampling and those tight binders. The method does two useful things. It turns the number of tight binders a model needs into the number of compounds a library must contain. And it estimates the expected yield of a randomly designed second-generation DEL, so the gain from designing the library with machine learning or active learning can be measured against a baseline, without the expense of a control library as large as the experiment itself.

### [5] [Expanding the Decision Space of Pretrained Models through Structured Re-Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.15109)
*Tinglan Wang,Qun Su,Shihang Wang,Chi Wang,Lin Wang,Shaobo Chen,Haixu Liu,Hai Wei,Yin Ma,Yu Kang,Qi Gao,Xiaojun Yao,Tingjun Hou,Kai Wen*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: SRD是一种无需训练的框架，通过重组局部候选来优化结构推理，在多个科学领域中展示了改进的性能。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 解决预训练模型在新结构约束下组合局部预测的限制问题。

Method: 引入结构化重新解码（SRD），通过构建局部候选、联合优化选择和验证输出来改进推理。

Result: SRD在分子生成、蛋白质设计等多个领域提升了结构推理性能，并支持硬件优化。

Conclusion: SRD展示了在科学领域中广泛应用的潜力，尤其是在量子启发的方法中。

Abstract: Native decoders limit how pretrained models combine local predictions under new structural constraints. We introduce Structured Re-Decoding (SRD), a training-free framework that constructs local candidates from model scores, samples, or external specifications, jointly optimizes their selection, and verifies reconstructed outputs. We show that candidate recombination expands feasible support and can improve attainable reward under a fixed KL divergence budget. Across molecular generation, protein design, RNA structure prediction, and antibody modeling, SRD enables improved structured inference, including verified molecules absent from the original sample pool. External chemical templates further extend SRD to noncanonical amino acids, peptide cyclization, and grammar-guided molecular construction. Preliminary experiments on a physical coherent Ising machine demonstrate the feasibility of hardware-based optimization, suggesting broader opportunities for quantum and quantum-inspired approaches to structured inference and combinatorial optimization across scientific domains.

</details>
