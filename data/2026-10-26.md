### [1] [Computational De Novo Design of Miniprotein Candidate Neutralizers Targeting the IL-23 Cytokine Subunit p19](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32779)
*Raghav Paladugu,Fardina Fathmiul Alam*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 该研究通过计算设计靶向IL-23 p19的小蛋白，克服了传统方法的局限性，并提出了新的评分指标ipSAE。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: IL-23在炎症性肠病和牛皮癣中起关键作用，目前抗体疗法成本高且需注射。小蛋白为替代方案，但尚未靶向IL-23细胞因子本身。

Method: 使用计算设计（BindCraft方法）和新的评分指标ipSAE评估小蛋白结合IL-23 p19的能力。

Result: 成功设计出42种高评分小蛋白，其中5种表现出结合潜在热点和良好能量学特性。

Conclusion: 研究首次提出计算设计的小蛋白候选物，但需实验验证有效性。

Abstract: Interleukin-23 (IL-23) drives T-helper-17 inflammation underlying inflammatory bowel disease and psoriasis; anti-p19 antibodies are clinically effective but large, costly, and injectable. De novo miniproteins are an attractive alternative, yet published designs target the IL-23 receptor; the IL-23 cytokine itself has not been addressed. A further obstacle is that the standard confidence score (ipTM) tracks construct length rather than interface quality. We computationally design miniprotein binders against the IL-23R-binding face of p19 (PDB 3DUH) and score every design with ipSAE, a length-robust interface metric derived from AlphaFold-initial-guess predicted aligned error. A conventional RFdiffusion/ProteinMPNN/ColabFold pipeline yields no reliable predicted interface across 972 designs (best ipSAE 0.30), and a scoring artifact, in which single-sequence ColabFold under-rates binder pLDDT by approximately 56 points, explains an earlier misleading "binders do not fold" signal. An interface-optimized hallucination method (BindCraft), scored identically, yields 42 of 50 designs above ipSAE 0.60 (best 0.804). An AlphaFold-independent geometric check reproduces the conventional failure: no top-ranked conventional design reaches the buried area or interface hydrogen-bond count of the weakest lead. Five all-alpha-helical leads (72-104 residues) collectively engage all six targeted hotspots; two dock within the intact p40/p19 heterodimer and all show favorable Rosetta energetics. A p40-alone counter-screen returns ipSAE 0.00 for all five, indicating no spurious p40 interface. To our knowledge, these are the first computationally proposed de novo miniprotein candidates designed to occlude the IL-23R-binding face of IL-23 p19. All results require experimental validation.

### [2] [What Transfers Across Protein Pockets? Experience Reuse in Molecular Optimization for Agentic Structure-Based Drug Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32620)
*Zaifei Yang,Weiyu Chen,Yaqing Wang,James Kwok*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: LLM agents通过逐步编辑和对接配体来优化蛋白质口袋中的配体，利用经验库继承先前探针的信息，但研究发现描述编辑和分子的信号比描述目标的信号更有助于分离改善和恶化的编辑。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究如何利用经验库（experience base）来继承和传递先前探针（probes）的信息，以提高新配体编辑的效率。

Method: 通过分析CrossDocked2020测试集中的蛋白质口袋，使用接触点、动作和轮次作为键（key），从经验库中查找和分离改善与恶化的编辑。

Result: 研究发现，编辑和分子的特征（如大小变化、动作和轮次）能更有效地分离改善与恶化的编辑，而具体目标描述的信号传递效果有限。

Conclusion: 经验库在当前表示和查找方案下，更适合用于优先排序代理自身的对接提案，而非直接指导构建新配体。

Abstract: LLM agents optimize a ligand in a protein pocket by editing it step by step and docking each edit. Recent agents first spend extra dockings probing the complex with controlled edits; guidance induced from these probes is reported to help more than the chemical knowledge the model already carries. The probes are then discarded with the instance that produced them, so the obvious next step is to keep them in an experience base, letting a new pocket inherit what earlier ones already paid for. Such a base is read through a \emph{key}: the record properties under which past edits are pooled and a new one is looked up. Using the full log of one such run over the CrossDocked2020 test-set pockets, we ask whether its keys recur on a pocket the base has never seen and still separate improving from worsening edits. \crchange{Using} the contacts at the site being edited, the action, and the round as \crchange{a} key, the base separates them sharply. Within our analysis, this separation is largely explained by how much the edit changed the molecule's size, the action, and the round, none of which describes the target. That is not because the target is irrelevant: naming it outright separates them several times more sharply. For a pocket absent from the base, transfer must rely on a description rather than its identity. Among the exact-match descriptions we evaluate, the more specific ones rarely recur, whereas the coarser ones provide little separation; the signals that do transfer primarily describe the edit and the molecule it produced rather than the target. Under the representations and lookup scheme studied here, an experience base may be more useful for prioritizing an agent's own proposals for docking than for directly prescribing what to build.

</details>
