### [1] [Evolutionary Informed Translation from Protein to Nucleic Acid Sequence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22562)
*Anna Hart,Mohammed El-Kebir,Huimin Zhao,Heng Ji*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: PINATA利用系统发育信息和多序列比对生成更自然的核酸序列，优于现有方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有蛋白质到核酸的翻译模型常孤立处理序列，过度偏向常用密码子，缺乏生物进化背景。

Method: 引入PINATA模型，利用多序列比对（MSAs）和进化关系，预测物种和蛋白质特异性序列。

Result: 在61,934个基因树和882个物种中评估，PINATA显著提高天然密码子恢复率17.95%，保留稀有密码子细节。

Conclusion: 利用进化数据的生物学习方法能更准确地跨物种翻译蛋白质到核酸序列。

Abstract: The choice of nucleic acid sequence for a given protein strongly impacts function and is species-specific. Current protein-to-nucleic-acid translation models treat sequences in isolation, often over-favoring frequently used codons. To generate more natural nucleic acid sequences, we introduce PINATA (Phylogenetically Informed protein to Nucleic Acid Translation with sequence Alignment), a model that leverages sequences from related species encoded in multiple sequence alignments (MSAs) to guide prediction. Our architecture leverages evolutionary context and learns species-and protein specific context to make biologically-informed predictions. Evaluated across 61,934 gene trees and 882 species, PINATA generates sequences that mirror the naturally-occurring sequence substantially better than existing language models. PINATA leverages MSA data to improve exact native codon recovery by 17.95 percentage points over existing language models, effectively preserves natural rare-codon nuances, and more closely matches native sequence properties. PINATA maintains strong predictive performance even with limited evolutionary data. While previous methods largely treated sequences from different species in isolation, our method leverages the evolutionary relationships between nucleic acids and proteins across species to create a more biologically-informed translation. We demonstrate that leveraging rich evolutionary data through an biologically-designed method strongly aids in translating protein sequences to nucleic acid sequences across species. We will make data and code publicly available upon publication.

### [2] [Multiscale Quantum-Classical Simulation of Protein Autoproteolysis in a Million-Atom Membrane Environment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23108)
*Alexander Orlov,Alexis Ralli,Tim Weaving,Michael Williams de la Bastida,Reuben Martin,Zohim Chandani,Minh Chung,P. V. Sriluckshmy,Fedor Simkovic,Luigi Iapichino,Peter J. Love,Christian B. Mendl,Andrea Townsend-Nicholson,Peter V. Coveney*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 提出了一种结合量子计算和经典计算的混合方法，用于生物分子中化学反应的高精度模拟。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 生物分子中的反应中心常处于复杂环境中，难以准确预测其能量和机制。

Method: 结合分子动力学、密度泛函理论和相关波函数方法，整合CPU、GPU和量子处理器（QPU），实现多尺度模拟。

Result: 成功模拟了ADGRG1的自蛋白水解反应，处理了包含百万原子的系统。

Conclusion: 量子计算机可作为科学工作流的加速器，提升电子结构方法在生物系统中的适用性。

Abstract: Predicting chemical reactions in biomolecules is challenging because reactive centers are embedded within complex environments that strongly influence their energetics and mechanisms. Here, we present a hybrid quantum-classical workflow that combines molecular dynamics, density functional theory, and correlated wavefunction methods, tightly integrating CPUs, GPUs, and quantum processing units (QPUs) to enable simulations across multiple length and accuracy scales. We apply this approach to ADGRG1 autoproteolysis, a maturation reaction in an adhesion G protein-coupled receptor that has not previously been studied using correlated electronic-structure methods. Our simulations treat a biologically realistic system containing more than one million atoms, including the full-length uncleaved receptor embedded in an endoplasmic-reticulum membrane, while incorporating a 54-qubit IQM quantum processor and up to 1,200 NVIDIA H100 GPUs. These results demonstrate how quantum computers can serve as accelerators within scalable scientific workflows, extending correlated electronic-structure methods to biologically relevant systems.

</details>
