### [1] [Exclusion and internal structure in the association/dissociation kinetics of DNA-binding proteins in the fast diffusion limit](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33740)
*Xiangting Li,Tom Chou*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 本文提出了一种新的粗粒度随机模型，用于描述DNA-蛋白质非特异性结合的动力学，考虑了蛋白质的构象转换和足迹大小，并验证了模型对RPA与ssDNA结合动力学的适用性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有理论缺乏对DNA结合蛋白在扩散和构象转换耦合动力学中的精确描述，尤其是快速扩散状态下。

Method: 开发了一种新的粗粒度随机模型，推导了有效的马尔可夫模型和平均场近似，考虑了蛋白质足迹和构象变化。

Result: 模型定量拟合了RPA与ssDNA的结合动力学，揭示了多段结构如何平衡高亲和性与快速重构。

Conclusion: 该模型适用于多种DNA结合蛋白系统，为实验数据提供了准确的预测和新的分析策略。

Abstract: In many canonical biophysical and biochemical contexts, complex diffusing DNA-binding proteins undergoing nonspecific adsorption and desorption can exist in different conformational states. Equilibrium binding theories and kinetic models of competing ligands provide a basis for describing these systems. Coupling the internal conformational kinetics of bound proteins to exclusion and diffusion requires tracking both protein positions and binding states. Closed-form, tractable mathematical models that accurately describe the stochastic dynamics of structured proteins that bind to and diffuse along DNA are lacking. Here, we develop a new coarse-grained stochastic model of nonspecific DNA-protein binding in the fast diffusion regime, from which we derive an effective Markov model and its corresponding mean-field approximation that incorporates protein footprint size and conformational switching. We fit our new model quantitatively to previously measured dynamics of Replication Protein A (RPA) binding to ssDNA. Finally, by including multisegment binding states, our analysis captures facilitated exchange, revealing how multisegment architecture balances high affinity with rapid remodeling. Our theoretical model applies broadly to many other DNA-binding protein systems, including nucleosomes, ssDNA-binding proteins, and other multisegment complexes, providing accurate predictions and new strategies for evaluating experimental data.

### [2] [KnitNet: Learning to Search at Inference Time for Exact RNA Inverse Design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33812)
*Antonia Panescu,Xin Wang,Haoran Xu,Qingyu Chen,Carlos Oliver,Rex Ying*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: KnitNet是一种新型RNA逆设计框架，通过验证器反馈优化序列以实现精确的折叠匹配，提高了计算效率和准确率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有RNA序列设计方法无法在训练分布之外实现精确结构匹配，或搜索成本过高，因此需要一种更高效的方法。

Method: KnitNet利用验证器反馈推断核苷酸依赖关系，调整联合修复策略，并通过在线代理预测有前景的编辑，逐步优化搜索过程。

Result: KnitNet在五个二级结构基准测试中平均提高21%的精确解决率，时间减少70%，首次在OpenKnot57中实现所有复杂伪结结构的精确匹配。

Conclusion: KnitNet不仅在二维结构设计中表现优异，还能推广到三维结构，显著提高自一致性并加速计算。

Abstract: RNA inverse design seeks nucleotide sequences that fold into specified target structures. Existing methods rely on learned generators that can miss exact structural matches, especially beyond their training distributions, or on costly search over a vast, multimodal sequence space. Search is further complicated by globally coupled nucleotide interactions, as correcting one pairing error can create new errors beyond a fixed repair scope. To address this, we introduce KnitNet, an inference-time scaling framework that learns from verifier feedback to refine sequences for exact fold-back matching. Starting from candidates generated for a target secondary structure, KnitNet uses mismatched folds to infer nucleotide dependencies and adapt its joint repair strategy, while an online surrogate learns to predict which edits are promising. This makes search progressively more informed and compute-efficient. Crucially, KnitNet generalizes across RNA structural regimes by changing only the optimization signal to match the verifier required by each setting. Across five secondary-structure benchmarks, KnitNet increases exact solve rates by an average of 21 percentage points over continued sampling with 70% less time-to-solve. On OpenKnot57 specifically, KnitNet is the first method to achieve exact computational matches for all complex, pseudoknotted structures. Beyond 2D, KnitNet improves 3D structural self-consistency over per-target RL by 7.8% while being 39.3% faster, demonstrating generalization and effective inference-time scaling across RNA design regimes.

### [3] [Quantum chaotic signature in non-empirical ensemble of DNA base pairs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.34305)
*Mehdi Ameri*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 研究了GC和AT碱基对的分子轨道能级统计行为，发现其符合半泊松分布，介于可积性和混沌之间。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探索多体混沌量子系统的统计行为，特别是短程能级排斥和长程谱刚性。

Method: 通过DFT计算碱基对的Kohn-Sham分子轨道能级，分析其概率分布、能隙比、数方差和谱压缩性。

Result: 轨道能级统计与半泊松分布吻合，表明系统介于可积性和混沌之间。

Conclusion: 潜在生物物理意义包括电荷转移等生物过程。

Abstract: The statistical study of many-body chaotic quantum systems would indicate important phenomena such as short-range level repulsion and long-range spectral rigidity. We investigate such behavior in an ensemble of gas-phase guanine-cytosine (GC) and adenine-thymine (AT) Watson-Crick (WC) base pairs by calculating their Kohn-Sham molecular orbital energy levels via ab initio density functional theory (DFT). We track the probability distribution, adjacent-gap ratios (AGR), number variance, and spectral compressibility of the unfolded level spacings to see whether their statistics follow the statistics of the eigenvalues of random matrices. After comparing the results to Poisson, semi-Poisson (SP), and Gaussian Orthogonal Ensemble (GOE) statistics, it becomes evident that the statistics of orbital energy levels agree closely with the semi-Poisson distribution, pointing to an intermediate regime between integrability and chaos, characterized by level repulsion and strong long-range correlations. Potential biophysical implications for charge transfer and related biological processes are also discussed.

### [4] [From the Deterministic Hook Effect to Stochastic Reliability in Ternary Complex Formation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33753)
*Deepak K. Agrawal*

Main category: math.DS

TL;DR: 研究探讨了在小型反应体积中，即使平均结合响应良好，也不一定能确保可靠组装的原因。通过确定性模型和随机模拟，发现体积和分子数阈值等因素会影响三元复合物的可靠形成。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探讨在小体积环境中，三元复合物形成的可靠性问题，尤其是当介质剂量变化时，确定性模型与实际情况的差异。

Method: 使用确定性方程、精确随机模拟和简单催化扩展模型，研究了一个最小可逆结合网络的行为。

Result: 确定性模型展示了钩形响应，而随机模拟显示小体积常无法积累足够复合物。体积、分子数阈值和反应速率影响可靠性。

Conclusion: 平均响应良好不保证小体积中的可靠组装，需考虑体积和动力学因素。

Abstract: Ternary binding systems occur in signaling, scaffold-mediated assembly, and targeted protein degradation. A mediator brings two partners into a productive ternary complex, but excess mediator can hold them in separate binary complexes. Consequently, ternary complex formation can increase and then decrease as the mediator dose increases, producing the hook effect. Deterministic dose-response curves describe the average behavior but do not indicate whether individual small compartments reliably form the complex. A minimal reversible binding network was studied using deterministic equations, exact stochastic simulations, and a simple catalytic extension. The deterministic model recovered the hook-shaped response. In stochastic simulations, small volumes often failed to accumulate the required number of complexes, even where the deterministic prediction was favorable. At low mediator doses, failure reflected a shortage of mediator molecules; at high doses, material accumulated in free or binary-bound forms. Larger volumes increased the range of mediator doses supporting reliable formation, while stricter copy-number thresholds narrowed that range. Faster ternary-complex completion or slower breakup improved reliability. In the catalytic model, product formation also depended on how early productive complexes formed. These results show why a favorable average binding response does not necessarily ensure reliable assembly in small reaction volumes.

</details>

### [5] [MolGPAO: Molecular Generative Pre-trained Autoregressive Octree with Speculative Decoding and Sub-Millisecond Seam Healing for 3D Molecules](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33985)

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: MolGPAO是一种自回归3D八叉树生成模型，提升了分子生成的几何平衡性和性能，计算效率优于现有模型。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有3D八叉树自回归生成模型（如Uni-3DAR）虽然计算效率高（O(N log N)），但在几何平衡性、闭壳层价态稳定性和性能保真度上表现不足。

Method: MolGPAO结合了多项创新技术：1) 非陷阱嵌入与共价堆积先验；2) FlashAttention-2暂停-聚集八叉树漏斗；3) 抗捷径的双阶段八叉树批评器；4) 快速的SE(3)后处理图优化；5) GPU叶节点解码。

We introduce MolGPAO (Molecular Generative Pre-trained Autoregressive Octree), a standalone 3D octree architecture and single-output speculative tree-decoding engine using zero external surrogates, zero reinforcement learning, and zero inference-time MLIP relaxation. MolGPAO unifies: (1) non-trapping 6-D ScaleFreeBudgetEmbedding with L5-to-L1 covalent packing priors; (2) a FlashAttention-2 pause-and-gather speculative octree funnel ($m_{s1} \to m_{s2} \to 1$, $M_{\mathrm{out}} = 1$); (3) an anti-shortcut ground-truth TwoStageOctreeProcessCritic with 64-RBF covalent edge gating and $SO(3)$-invariant 12-channel multipole norms (blind QM9 $r = 0.9867$--$0.9989$); (4) a $<0.5$ ms 4-layer $SE(3)$ PostTreeGraphRefiner for Morton-seam healing, expected-XYZ bonded supervision, selective CFG, and zero-heavy-displacement valence reconciliation; and (5) bubble-free stride-2 GPU leaf decoding. Across GEOM-Drugs (0.49 GPU-h), disjoint 50k/50k QM9 (0.43 GPU-h), and PBE0/def2-TZVP-verified OE62 (0.56 GPU-h), MolGPAO lifts drug-scale valid-connected yield 6.78x (12.7% to 86.2%, 99.83% atom stability), runs 7.3x faster (or 1.65x faster at 2.0x yield) than 100-step FlowMol at $N=100$, beats 156 GPU-h PropMolFlow on QM9 HOMO (-12.7%) and dipole (-21.0%) at 362x lower compute, and doubles live DFT hit rate on OE62 (33.3% vs. 16.7%) at 92.8x lower compute.
