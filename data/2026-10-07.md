### [1] [Mathematical Invariant-Enabled Topological Neural Networks for Molecular and Materials Property Prediction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07712)
*Yiming Ren,Xiang Liu,Mustafa Hajij,Pietro Liò,Guo-Wei Wei*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: MITNNs是一套结合多种数学视角和拓扑神经架构的框架，通过多尺度不变量捕捉复杂结构的互补信息，在多个预测任务中优于现有方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有分子和材料学习方法通常依赖有限的结构表示，可能仅捕捉复杂三维结构的某些方面。

Method: 引入MITNNs框架，结合拓扑、谱理论、交换代数、微分几何和离散曲率的多尺度不变量，与拓扑神经架构集成。

Result: MITNNs在蛋白质-配体结合、金属-有机框架性质、突变诱导蛋白质溶解度和分子毒性预测等任务中优于现有方法。

Conclusion: MITNNs成为一种数学多模态的科学机器学习框架。

Abstract: Existing molecular and materials learning approaches often rely on a limited set of structural representations, which may capture only selected aspects of complex three-dimensional structure. Here, we introduce mathematical invariant-enabled topological neural networks (MITNNs), a framework that represents complex structures through multiple complementary mathematical views and integrates them with topological neural architectures. MITNNs combine multiscale invariants from topology, spectral theory, commutative algebra, differential geometry, and discrete curvature, capturing complementary structural information from the same system. Systematic invariant-subset, architecture-subset, and ensemble analyses show that predictive performance depends on how mathematical representations and neural architectures are paired, with selected combinations outperforming individual models and the aggregation of all available components. Across protein-ligand binding, metal-organic framework properties, mutation-induced protein solubility, and molecular toxicity prediction, MITNN consistently outperforms existing methods. These results establish MITNN as a mathematically multimodal framework for scientific machine learning.

### [2] [Information-Dense Synthesis for Molecular Discovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.08495)
*Kasper K. Jakobsen,Eli N. Weinstein*

Main category: stat.ML

TL;DR: 该论文提出了一种通过算法控制随机合成的方法，高效搜索分子空间，减少实验次数，显著优于传统方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 针对分子设计中稀有特性分子难以发现的问题，传统算法在稀疏环境下效果不佳，需改进搜索效率。

Method: 通过设计和合成复杂混合物，整体测试后解卷积分子活性关系，优化合成以编码最大信息。

Result: 理论显示实验次数从$О(d)$降至$О(лоg d)$或$О(1)$，模拟中比贝叶斯优化方法少用十倍实验找到活性分子。

Conclusion: 该方法显著提高分子发现效率，适用于稀疏特性分子搜索。

Abstract: Machine learning can accelerate molecular discovery by designing molecules and planning experiments. However, many scientific challenges demand molecules with very rare properties, and in this sparse setting, existing algorithms offer little gain over random guessing. We propose a method to efficiently search large regions of molecular space using algorithmically controlled stochastic synthesis. Rather than design, make and test individual molecules, we design and make complex mixtures, test them as a pool, then deconvolute the molecule-activity map. We optimize synthesis to encode maximal information. Theoretically, this approach can reduce the number of experiments required to find the optimal molecule among $d$ candidates from $\mathcal{O}(d)$ to $\mathcal{O}(\log d)$ or $\mathcal{O}(1)$. In simulation, on estimated protein fitness landscapes, it finds active molecules with an order of magnitude fewer experiments than existing Bayesian optimization methods.

### [3] [IMPPAT 3.0: An updated FAIR database of phytochemicals and formulations of Indian Medicinal plants](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07478)
*Shanmuga Priya Baskaran,Ajaya Kumar Sahoo,Priyotosh Sil,Rahul Tiwari,Nikhil Chivukula,Sabrina Elsa Eapen,Geetha Ranganathan,Preeti Semwal,Areejit Samal*

Main category: q-bio.MN

TL;DR: IMPPAT 3.0是一个更新的数据库，整合了阿育吠陀配方的信息，并扩展了药用植物、植物化学成分及治疗用途的覆盖范围，为传统医药知识的系统研究提供了平台。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 阿育吠陀配方蕴含丰富的传统知识，但与现代生物医学术语和资源的系统整合仍有限。

Method: IMPPAT 3.0通过整合权威来源的阿育吠陀配方信息，并扩展对药用植物、植物化学成分等的数据标注，构建了一个知识图谱（IMPPAT-KG）。

Result: 通过网络医学分析，资源展示了其在单草药治疗类风湿性关节炎中的应用潜力。

Conclusion: IMPPAT 3.0为传统医药知识的系统研究和药物发现提供了FAIR兼容的平台。

Abstract: Ayurvedic formulations represent a rich source of traditional knowledge, encompassing distinct therapeutic approaches, medicinal ingredients, and underlying principles of drug action. However, systematic integration of this knowledge with standardized modern biomedical terminology and resources remains limited. Here, we present IMPPAT 3.0, an updated version of the Indian Medicinal Plants, Phytochemistry And Therapeutics (IMPPAT: https://cb.imsc.res.in/imppat/) database that extends its scope by incorporating curated and harmonized information on Ayurvedic formulations from authoritative sources, while expanding its coverage of medicinal plants, phytochemicals, and therapeutic use terms. IMPPAT 3.0 further enriches plant and phytochemical information with annotations on clinical trials, patents, bioactivity, protein targets, spectral data, plant image links, and AyuCaRe reports. The compiled data is also organized as IMPPAT-KG, a knowledge graph that enables exploration of relationships among IMPPAT entities. We demonstrate the utility of the resource through a network medicine analysis of single herbal drugs (SHDs) reported for rheumatoid arthritis (RA), revealing significant proximity of their protein targets to RA-associated genes, and pinpointing of phytochemical pairs with potentially complementary effects on RA pathogenesis. In conclusion, by integrating traditional medicinal knowledge, IMPPAT provides a FAIR-compliant ethnopharmacological platform for systematic investigation of Indian medicinal plants and Ayurvedic formulations for drug discovery applications.
