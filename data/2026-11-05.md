### [1] [Discrete Backbone Elasticity and Curvature-Dependent Torsion Statistics in Proteins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.06363)
*Yiquan Wang,Mingyu Bian,Jiayuan Ma*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 论文提出了一种基于离散Hasimoto框架的蛋白质构象动力学粗粒度模型，通过弯曲-扭转相互作用和广义von Mises密度优化参数，显著提升了构象预测的准确性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究旨在测试局部链弹性对蛋白质构象有序性的影响，并开发一种更准确的统计模型来描述蛋白质主链的几何特征。

Method: 使用离散Hasimoto框架连接邻近弯曲失配，推导出弯曲-扭转相互作用和六参数广义von Mises密度，并在ATLAS分子动力学数据库的194条链上进行拟合。

Result: 模型将负对数密度损失从1.490降低到1.160纳特，扭转符号错误从12.26%降至2.15%，螺旋分数错误从21.84%降至10.41%。进一步扩展各向异性刚度和双态弹性模型后，测试损失降至0.790纳特，螺旋分数错误降至1.65%。

Conclusion: 研究结果将离散Hasimoto弹性与统计骨架势能联系起来，明确了局部Cα几何的适用范围和限制。

Abstract: Coarse-grained models of protein conformational dynamics use $\mathrm{C}_α$ geometry to describe the probabilities of regular helices, ordered turns and disordered conformations. To test how much of this order arises from local chain elasticity, we connect discrete Hasimoto frame transport to neighboring bending mismatch, deriving a curvature-product torsion interaction and a six-parameter generalized von Mises density. Fitted on 194 chains from the ATLAS molecular dynamics database and tested on 1744 sequence-nonredundant proteins, this baseline reduces negative log-density loss from 1.490 to 1.160 nats, torsion-sign error from 12.26 to 2.15 percentage points, and helix-fraction error from 21.84 to 10.41 points. Ablations and circular-moment projections against reference generative models motivate extensions to anisotropic stiffness and two partition-function-weighted elastic states, further lowering test loss to 0.790 nats and helix error to 1.65 points. The fixed hierarchy also improves predictions across 72 trajectories of peptides $\mathrm{Fs}$ and $(\mathrm{AAQAA})_3$ and miniprotein $\mathrm{HP35}$ spanning varied starting conformations and force fields. Remaining angular errors in these trajectories are associated with atomic context at shared bends, as bend-matched peptide frames adopt opposite torsion signs with distinct hydrogen-bond patterns and a proline-containing $\mathrm{HP35}$ turn remains at negative torsion. Introducing a bilinearly coupled $\mathrm{C}_α$--$\mathrm{N}$ orientation angle largely reparameterizes the existing bend-dependent harmonics, with an empirical $L^2$ Gram projection placing 96.7\% to 99.6\% of its added coefficient-function squared norm within the original six-function span. These results link discrete Hasimoto elasticity to statistical backbone potentials and clarify the scope and limits of local $\mathrm{C}_α$ geometry.
