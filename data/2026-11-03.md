### [1] [Automatic Generation of Novel Highly-Functional Molecular Sequences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03603)
*Vincent R. Ragusa,Chrisoph Adami*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 生成功能性序列变体的方法很多，但能否生成超出示例集功能的序列尚不明确。研究比较了变分自编码器和信息论方法，发现后者更高效且效果更好。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探讨生成方法是否能发现超越已知示例的高功能序列。

Method: 使用两个完整的适应性景观，比较变分自编码器和信息论方法在搜索序列空间中的效果。

Result: 信息论方法无需训练，运行更快，且在发现稀有高功能序列上更有效。

Conclusion: 信息论方法可用于发现具有特殊抗细菌特性的稀有多肽。

Abstract: While multiple methods now exist to generate functional sequence variants from existing annotated examples, it is unclear how well such methods can be used to generate sequences that exceed the functionality of those contained in the example set. Using two complete fitness landscapes, we quantify how effectively generative tools can search sequence space and find rare, novel, highly functional sequences, focusing on variational auto-encoders and information-theoretic methods. We find that an information-theoretic method that does not require training and runs significantly faster than standard Machine-Learning/Artificial Intelligence (ML/AI) methods also is significantly more effective in finding rare highly-functional sequences. We discuss the application of this novel method to discover rare polypeptides with anti-bacterial properties that are specific to bacterial, but not eukaryotic, cell membranes.

### [2] [Canonic hemoglobin-oxygen equilibrium: Reassessing the role of cooperativity](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00824)
*A. E. Allahverdyan,S. G. Gevorkian,A. Harutyunyan,Y. Sh. Mamasakhlisov,V. Stepanyan*

Main category: physics.bio-ph

TL;DR: 论文探讨了红血球内血红蛋白-氧平衡的模型，指出传统的大正则系综假设不适用，提出了基于固定体积和温度的规范系综模型，并重新推导了Pauling和Monod-Wyman-Changeux模型，发现协作能较低且氧结合波动更小。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统的大正则系综模型假设血红蛋白浸泡在未结合氧分子的血浆中，但红血球内血红蛋白浓度远高于血浆中未结合氧浓度，这一假设不成立，因此需要更准确的模型。

Method: 提出基于固定体积和温度的规范系综模型，重新推导了Pauling和Monod-Wyman-Changeux模型，比较了大正则系综与规范系综的预测结果。

Result: 规范系综模型预测的协作能显著低于大正则系综，且氧结合波动更小；大正则系综预测的高协作能可能导致氧释放的不稳定性。

Conclusion: 规范系综模型更适合红血球内血红蛋白-氧平衡的研究，且氧在血红蛋白间的跳跃扩散可能在红血球内氧扩散中起重要作用。

Abstract: Hemoglobin-oxygen equilibrium is normally studied within the grand canonical ensemble, which assumes that each hemoglobin is immersed in a reservoir of unbound oxygen molecules of the plasma. We show that this assumption is incorrect inside the RBC (red blood cell or erythrocyte), where the hemoglobin concentration is larger than the concentration of unbound oxygen in plasma. We suggest a better model, where a single hemoglobin and a few oxygen molecules around it reach a canonical equilibrium at a fixed volume (determined from the RBC structure) and a fixed temperature. The basic models of hemoglobin-oxygen equilibrium -- Pauling's model and Monod-Wyman-Changeux model -- can be reformulated for this canonical situation. They predict cooperative interaction energies, which are significantly lower than predictions of the same models in the grand canonical ensemble. Larger cooperative energies, in particular those predicted by the grand canonical ensemble, lead to instabilities (cascade processes) in oxygen release within the canonical approach. Oxygen-binding fluctuations within this approach are sizably smaller than those in the grand canonical situation. These results suggest that the hopping diffusion of oxygen from one hemoglobin to another may play a role in oxygen diffusion within the RBC.

### [3] [PeptSol: A Translational Benchmark for Protected-Peptide Solubility in Drug Manufacturing](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00749)
*Alexander Arjun Lobo,Marc Saouda,Urvi Awasthi,Leonid Zhukov*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: PeptSol是一个包含1388个保护性肽溶解度测量的开放基准数据集，用于评估分子表示模型在液相肽合成中的表现。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有肽性质基准数据集中缺乏保护基、末端修饰和非规范残基的充分表征，限制了分子表示模型的开发和评估。

Method: 引入了PeptSol数据集，包含1388个手工整理的溶解度测量数据，并采用化学感知的数据划分。测试了七种分子编码器的性能。

Result: 最佳模型在二分类任务中达到0.843的F1分数，三分类任务中为0.614。化学指纹结合分子描述符的基线模型表现稳定。

Conclusion: PeptSol为合成感知的肽可开发性研究设立了新标准，模型性能因任务和数据划分而异。

Abstract: Liquid-phase peptide synthesis (LPPS) offers a scalable route to peptide manufacturing, but route development still depends on empirical solvent screens because each protected intermediate must be soluble for reaction and recoverable for purification. The protecting groups, terminal modifications, and noncanonical residues that govern this behaviour are poorly represented in existing peptide property benchmarks, limiting the ability to develop and rigorously evaluate molecular representation models for LPPS-relevant prediction tasks. We introduce PeptSol, an open benchmark of 1388 hand-curated protected-peptide solubility measurements with chemistry-aware splits. The measurements span only 84 parent sequences and 101 protected structures, and 92.5% come from a single historical programme. Across seven frozen molecular encoders, the best macro-F1 is 0.843 on binary solubility for the historical subset under a peptide-held-out split; the harder three-class ordinal task reaches 0.614 in that cell. No foundation model consistently outperforms a baseline combining chemical fingerprints with explicit molecular descriptors, and model rankings vary across prediction tasks and data splits. The dataset, evaluation protocol, and baselines establish a standard for measuring progress on molecular representations for synthesis-aware peptide developability.

### [4] [RangeFinder: Predicting Ligand Property Ranges from Protein Pockets](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03379)
*Alan Nadelsticher Ruvalcaba,Chris Alvin,Supratik Mukhopadhyay,Adam Bess*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: RangeFinder是一种SE(3)-等变变换口袋级模型，能直接从口袋结构和化学环境预测配体性质的范围，显著缩小配体搜索空间并优于现有方法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 解决现有机器学习方法在预测蛋白质-配体结合时缺乏关于化学空间信息的局限性。

Method: 使用SE(3)-等变变换器模型，基于BioLiP2数据训练，预测配体的九个物理化学性质范围。

Result: RangeFinder在range-IoU和Reduction-Recall F-Score指标上优于所有可部署基线，且计算速度快。

Conclusion: RangeFinder是一种高效且低成本的分子筛选工具，可显著缩小配体搜索空间。

Abstract: Identifying ligands for a protein target requires searching a vast chemical space for a comparatively small set of compatible molecules. Existing machine learning approaches primarily address this problem by generating candidate ligands or scoring individual protein-ligand pairs, but provide limited information about the broader chemical space compatible with a binding pocket. We introduce RangeFinder, an SE(3)-equivariant transformer pocket-level model, trained and evaluated on BioLiP2 data, that predicts ranges over nine physicochemical ligand properties directly from pocket structure, chemistry, and evolutionary context. Using an intersection-over-union objective and evaluation metric (range-IoU), RangeFinder achieves a range-IoU of $0.464 \pm 0.002$, outperforming all deployable baselines while requiring only $12.2$ ms per pocket. We also quantify our method's ability to reduce the ligand search space while retaining compatible binders; in this regard, RangeFinder achieves a Reduction-Recall F-Score of $0.543 \pm 0.011$ --- once again outperforming all deployable baselines. Ablation experiments indicate that RangeFinder's performance depends more strongly on the density and quality of ligand supervision than on any individual input modality or specialized training strategy. Together, these results position RangeFinder as an inexpensive and effective molecular filtering prior, with demonstrated potential in narrowing the ligand search space of target pockets before docking, generation, or synthesis. The code for this project can be found at: https://github.com/alannadels/RangeFinder.

</details>
