### [1] [Confident and Wrong: Structure Confidence Is a Poor Selection Filter for Protein Function](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17391)
*Eric LoPrete,Pavle Jeremić*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 基于结构指导的蛋白质设计可以生成候选蛋白质，但结构置信度并不能预测功能。通过功能指导的模型设计稀有地球元素结合蛋白变体，比结构置信度选择的变体表现更好。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究旨在验证结构置信度是否能预测蛋白质功能，并探索功能指导的蛋白质设计方法是否优于结构指导。

Method: 训练了一个功能指导的模型，用于设计稀有地球元素结合的蛋白质变体，并与结构置信度选择的设计进行比较。

Result: 功能指导的设计在结合能力上显著优于结构置信度选择的设计，且在选择性上也超过天然蛋白质家族。

Conclusion: 结构置信度仅代表设计的合理性，而直接基于功能测量的监督更能有效筛选设计。

Abstract: Structure-guided protein design can generate plausible candidates, but structural confidence does not predict function. We trained a function-guided model on measured metal-binding affinity with protein sequence, ligand identity, and assay conditions, and used it to design rare earth element (REE) binding variants of lanmodulin, a lanthanide-binding protein with four EF-hand domains. We screened 297 designs from three strategies against terbium and dysprosium, adjacent lanthanides among the hardest REE pairs to separate. Selection on structural confidence inverted the outcome it was meant to improve. The Structure-Confidence-Selected panel carried the highest pTM, complex ipLDDT, and composite confidence scores, and expressed at 97%, yet only 16.7% of its designs bound terbium (95% CI 10.4 to 25.7), against 83.3% (74.6 to 89.5) for the function-guided panel, whose confidence scores were the lowest. The odds of binding differed 25-fold between the two panels (Fisher exact $p = 1 \times 10^{-20}$). No structural-confidence metric separated binders from non-binders consistently across panels, whereas the function-trained model's predicted pKd gave the highest ROC AUC in every panel, 0.79 to 0.92, and ranked measured affinity among function-guided binders at Spearman $ρ= 0.98$. On the design objective, Tb/Dy selectivity, the function-guided designs also exceeded the natural lanmodulin family, outranking a randomly drawn ortholog 84% of the time across 149 orthologs. Structural confidence is a plausibility signal, not a selection criterion for function; direct supervision on measured function is what ranks designs.

### [2] [Deciphering multiple binding modes in large protein complexes via Enhanced Sampling and beta-VAE: the CYFIP1-eIF4E case](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18236)
*Francesco Pignotti,Emanuele Telari,Edoardo Milanetti,Anna La Teana,Jesmina Rexha,Alice Romagnoli,Luca Maragliano,Giovanni Di Muccio*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 本研究通过分子动力学模拟和数据分析，揭示了CYFIP1与eIF4E的多种结合模式，解释了其翻译抑制与细胞骨架功能的竞争机制。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 此前研究仅关注了CYFIP1的短螺旋基序，但其与eIF4E的完整界面未得到表征。本研究旨在填补这一空白。

Method: 结合全原子模拟、HADDOCK3数据驱动对接和粗粒化Martini 3模型，通过温度加速分子动力学增强采样，分析了100μs的轨迹。

Result: 发现CYFIP1通过三个主要模式与eIF4E结合，涉及背侧、侧向及双重位点，并揭示了其翻译与细胞骨架功能的竞争机制。

Conclusion: 这种异质、可塑的结合模式为突变研究和治疗性肽设计提供了结构基础。

Abstract: Eukaryotic initiation factor 4E (eIF4E) drives cap-dependent translation, regulated by 4E-binding proteins (4E-BPs) competing for its dorsal and lateral surfaces. CYFIP1, a scaffold protein, is both a WAVE Regulatory Complex component and a non-canonical 4E-BP repressing translation via a short helical motif, linked to autism spectrum disorders and cancer. Prior work modeled only this isolated motif as a peptide, leaving the full interface uncharacterized. Starting from this preliminary evidence, we modeled the full-length proteins as flexible partners: all-atom simulations fed a data-driven docking search (HADDOCK3), which guided funnel-restrained Temperature-Accelerated Molecular Dynamics of a coarse-grained Martini 3 representation. Ten replicas accumulated 100 $μ$s of enhanced sampling. Funnel-restraint variables efficiently sample binding and unbinding but are structurally degenerate: direct clustering of the raw trajectory collapses these poses into poorly resolved groups. We instead reweighted the biased trajectories onto a contact-based latent space built by benchmarking PCA against a beta-Variational Autoencoder, then clustered minima of the equilibrium free-energy surface: only intermediate regularization resolved distinct, homogeneous modes. Three such modes recur, engaging eIF4E's canonical dorsal site, its non-canonical lateral site, and both. We find that CYFIP1 also engages eIF4E through a second surface, the same that mediates its WAVE-complex dimerization, and a basic patch. Both plausibly explain residual binding previously reported after motif mutation. This suggests its translational and cytoskeletal roles compete for the same surface. Most contacts on eIF4E fall outside this dorsal/lateral interface. This heterogeneous, plastic ensemble offers a structural basis for mutagenesis and therapeutic peptide design.

### [3] [CARVE: A glycan-specific framework for assessing protein-glycan binding-pose quality](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18259)
*Yifei Xiao,Huifeng Zhao,Silong Zhai,Zichang Jin,Tingjun Hou,Yu Kang*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: CARVE是一种用于评估蛋白质-聚糖结构预测的聚糖特异性框架，结合了多种指标和有效性检查，为标准化的评估提供了基础。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有的蛋白质-聚糖相互作用评估方法通常依赖于固定的RMSD阈值，未能充分考虑聚糖的结构多样性和构象灵活性。

Method: CARVE通过拓扑感知的聚糖解析，整合了oRMSD、irRMSD和Fnat等指标，定义了四种质量类别和连续的CARVE-Score。

Result: CARVE在25,068个独立生成的模型上验证了其分类能力（macro-F1 = 0.892），并在CARVE-Bench上评估了九种预测方法。

Conclusion: CARVE为蛋白质-聚糖结构预测方法的评估提供了标准化工具，并有望推动更准确的对接和共折叠方法的开发。

Abstract: Protein-glycan interactions depend on the precise three-dimensional arrangement of carbohydrates at protein-binding interfaces, yet reliable assessment of predicted complex structures remains challenging. Existing evaluations commonly rely on fixed root-mean-square deviation (RMSD) thresholds, which do not fully account for the structural diversity and conformational flexibility of glycans. Here we developed CARVE, a glycan-specific framework for evaluating protein-glycan structure predictions. Through topology-aware glycan parsing, CARVE integrates orientation RMSD (oRMSD), interface-ring RMSD (irRMSD) and the fraction of native contacts (Fnat) to assess glycan scaffold geometry, interface-ring placement and native-contact recovery. These descriptors define four quality categories (CARVE-Level) with geometric thresholds that scale with the degree of polymerization (DP), and a continuous CARVE-Score, which reproduced those categories on 25,068 independently generated poses without recalibration (macro-F1 = 0.892). Pairing these measures with glycan-specific topological, chemical and geometric validity checks, we benchmarked nine prediction methods on CARVE-Bench, comprising 1,974 experimentally determined protein-glycan complexes. Among the top 25 predictions per target, HADDOCK3 achieved the highest Acceptable-level success rate (47.0%), whereas no method produced a high-quality, structurally valid pose for more than one in five. Collectively, CARVE provides a standardized basis for assessing protein-glycan structure prediction methods and guiding the development of more accurate docking and co-folding approaches.

### [4] [Mechanism-Level Chemical Reaction Simulation with Electron Bookkeeping Transformer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.17548)
*Sarah Cao,Shitong Luo,Connor W. Coley*

Main category: cs.CE

TL;DR: Bookkeeper是一个数据驱动模型，通过生成电子运动轨迹模拟化学反应机制，提高了反应步骤和路径预测的准确性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 化学反应的机理建模对于理解和预测反应性至关重要。

Method: Bookkeeper将每个电子建模为令牌，按顺序预测其运动，确保质量和电荷守恒。

Result: 在平衡测试集上，Bookkeeper在多个指标上优于FlowER，但在完整测试集上路径准确性较低。

Conclusion: 显式电子簿记是一个有用的框架，适用于虚拟筛选和验证合成计划预测。

Abstract: Modeling chemical reaction mechanisms is central to understanding and predicting chemical reactivity. We present Electron Bookkeeping Transformer (Bookkeeper), a data-driven model that simulates chemical reactions at mechanistic resolution by generating electron movement trajectories. Following the symbolic arrow-pushing formalism in chemistry, Bookkeeper explicitly models each electron as a token and predicts its movement sequentially, ensuring mass and charge conservation during both elementary step and reaction pathway predictions by construction. On reaction-size-balanced test sets, Bookkeeper improves top-1 elementary-step accuracy, top-1 pathway rank accuracy, and top-1 to top-5 product prediction accuracy over FlowER. On the entire test set, it achieves higher elementary-step top-1 accuracy but lower pathway accuracy than FlowER. Bookkeeper also samples elementary steps substantially faster, adapts to several unseen reaction types with minimal fine-tuning, and generates candidate chemical transformations for further screening. These results suggest that explicit electron bookkeeping is a useful framework for virtually screening chemical reactions across diverse reagents, validating computer-assisted synthesis planning predictions, and providing mechanistic interpretations of reactivity.

</details>
