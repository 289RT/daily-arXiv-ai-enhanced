### [1] [RNADyn: A Benchmark for Generating and Understanding RNA Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03712)
*Yiming Huang,Lennart Bastian,Hanqun Cao,Luis Vollmers,Tolga Birdal*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 论文介绍了RNADynBench和RNADynNet，一个标准化的RNA分子动力学基准测试和一个统一的学习RNA动力学的模型。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: RNA的功能通过构象变化实现，但静态结构无法完全捕捉这些变化，且现有方法将轨迹生成和动力学理解作为独立目标。

Method: RNADynBench提供2585个100纳秒的全原子轨迹，RNADynNet通过坐标去噪、单帧到轨迹对齐和物理接地，统一了轨迹生成和动力学指纹提取。

Result: 生成的轨迹在测试集中RMSF相关性达到0.875和0.766，单构象预测与MD衍生的动力学表现相当。

Conclusion: RNADynBench和RNADynNet为RNA动力学的生成和理解提供了基准和统一建模框架。

Abstract: Ribonucleic acid (RNA) functions through conformational changes that are not fully captured by static structures. However, large-scale standardized RNA dynamics data remain limited, and existing approaches typically treat trajectory generation and dynamics understanding as separate objectives. Here, we introduce RNADynBench, a standardized RNA molecular dynamics (MD) benchmark with 2585 quality-controlled 100-ns all-atom trajectories and leakage-controlled splits. Building on RNADynBench, we develop RNADynNet, a unified model for RNA dynamics learning that uses a shared backbone for both trajectory generation and dynamics fingerprint extraction from a single conformer. It combines coordinate denoising, single-frame-to-trajectory alignment, and physical grounding to connect all-atom trajectory generation with dynamics representation learning. Physical grounding improves both generated dynamics and the physical information recoverable from these fingerprints. Across both test sets, including the high-flexibility challenge set, the generated trajectories achieve RMSF correlations of 0.875 and 0.766, while single-conformer predictions show comparable agreement with MD-derived dynamics. RNADynBench and RNADynNet together establish a benchmark and unified modeling framework for generating and understanding RNA dynamics.
