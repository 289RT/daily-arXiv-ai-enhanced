### [1] [Biases and Limits of AlphaFold Predictions for Non-Globular Proteins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24546)
*Maria Cristina Aspromonte,Alexander M. Monzon,Miguel A. Andrade-Navarro,Oriol Bárcenas,Viktor Bartosik,Pau Bernadó,Juan Cortés,Zsuzsanna Dosztanyi,Gabor Erdos,Isabella C. Felli,Helena Gradišar,San Hadži,Tamás Hegedűs,Khalil Joron,Pavel Kaderavek,Eitan Lerner,Dirk Linke,Pablo Mier,Ana Pantelić,Roberta Pierattelli,Salvador Ventura,Marija Vidović,Metaxia Vlassi,Lukas Zidek,Silvio C. E. Tosatto*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: AlphaFold 在蛋白质结构预测方面取得了突破性进展，但研究者通过实验验证非球状蛋白质的结构差异，以指导更准确的模型解读。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探索 AlphaFold 预测与生物相关结构状态的差异及其分子背景，以提高模型的应用价值。

Method: 研究者通过实验验证非球状蛋白质的结构，分析 AlphaFold 预测与实际结构的差异。

Result: 发现 AlphaFold 预测在某些非球状蛋白上存在偏差，并揭示了分子背景对这些差异的影响。

Conclusion: 研究强调结合实验数据来更准确地解读 AlphaFold 模型，尤其是在非球状蛋白质领域。

Abstract: AlphaFold has transformed protein structure prediction, making structural models accessible across the life sciences. We explore experimentally validated cases of non-globular proteins to uncover where predictions diverge from biologically relevant structural states and how molecular context shapes these discrepancies, guiding a more informed interpretation of AlphaFold models.

### [2] [Micrograph B-factor estimation via Wilson statistics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24150)
*Ayelet Heimowitz,Amit Singer*

Main category: eess.IV

TL;DR: 提出一种预处理阶段确定和校正B因子的新方法，从而提高重建分辨率。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 传统的B因子校正作为3D重建的后处理步骤，假设所有实验图像共享同一B因子，缺乏灵活性。

Method: 将B因子的确定和校正作为重建的预处理步骤，而非后处理。

Result: 该方法能够提高重建分辨率。

Conclusion: 预处理阶段校正B因子是一种有效的改进方法。

Abstract: The single-particle cryo-electron microscopy imaging process is known to cause exponential decay in the amplitude and power spectra of proteins. This decay is modeled by the B factor and is currently corrected as a post-processing stage of the 3D reconstruction. As a consequence, all experimental images in the dataset are assumed to share the same B factor. In this paper, we suggest a novel approach wherein the B factor is determined and corrected as a preprocessing step to the reconstruction. We show such an approach can improve reconstruction resolution.

</details>
