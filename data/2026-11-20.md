### [1] [ShEPhERD-score: Fast, adaptable three-dimensional alignment of molecular features](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25076)
*Nate Somers,Kento A. Abeywardane,Connor W. Coley,Masha Elkin*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 论文提出了ShEPhERD-score，一个统一的、模块化的平台，用于三维分子相似性评分和对齐，结合了多种分子表示和高性能计算支持。

Motivation: 现有开源方法在分子相似性评分和对齐方面存在实现分散、API不一致、GPU支持有限等问题，难以比较、组合或扩展。

Method: 通过结合多种分子表示和高性能Numba CPU及Triton GPU内核，开发了ShEPhERD-score平台。

Result: 在DUDE-Z虚拟筛选基准测试中，ShEPhERD-score在九种情况下表现相当或优于其他工具，吞吐量高达每秒1.03百万次对齐。

Conclusion: ShEPhERD-score为虚拟筛选和机器学习工作流程提供了一个单一、可扩展的工具包，强调了统一平台的重要性。

Abstract: Three-dimensional (3D) molecular similarity scoring and alignment are widely employed in ligand-based virtual screening and molecular design, and are increasingly used for training and evaluating machine learning models. Existing open-source methods utilize various molecular representations spanning shape, electrostatics, pharmacophores, and hybrid representations, but their implementations as independent software packages with distinct application programming interfaces (APIs), optimizers, and limited GPU support make them difficult to compare, combine, or extend. Here, we develop ShEPhERD-score into a unified, modular platform for differentiable 3D molecular similarity scoring and alignment by combining diverse molecular representations with high-performance Numba CPU and Triton GPU kernels to support both large-scale library screening and pairwise similarity workflows. In matched comparisons with open-source 3D similarity tools on the DUDE-Z virtual screening benchmark, ShEPhERD-score performed comparably or better in eight of nine cases. Across the scoring modes in ShEPhERD-score, throughput ranges from 17,800 to 1.03 million alignments/second for ten-million-conformer libraries using a single NVIDIA L40S GPU. Moreover, we found that the most effective scoring mode varied by protein target, indicating that target-specific calibration against validation data may be valuable before large-scale screening and underscoring the need for a unified platform that provides diverse molecular representations. Together, these capabilities establish ShEPhERD-score as a single, extensible toolkit for rapid virtual screening and machine learning workflows.
