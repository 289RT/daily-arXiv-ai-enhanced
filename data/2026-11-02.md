### [1] [Contribution of chromosome-naïve transposon-seeded network of DNA templates to intracellular pools of protein dimers of liquid-liquid phase-separated condensate-forming proteins](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41029)
*Gennadi Glinsky*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 研究揭示蛋白-蛋白相互作用（PPI）级联在膜无细胞凝聚体形成中的作用，发现LLPS蛋白及其致病突变变体在EMC的HAP途径中富集。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探究PPI在膜无细胞凝聚体形成中的分子机制及其与人类疾病的关联。

Method: 结合AlphaFold 3D建模、突变模式分析和实验结构研究，鉴定PPI级联成分及其在HAP中的作用。

Result: PPI级联成分显著影响凝聚体和结构多蛋白复合体的形成，发现与人类病理相关的蛋白异源二聚体。

Conclusion: 研究提出PPI级联在凝聚体形成中的关键作用，为后续机制研究提供潜在基础和转化价值。

Abstract: Molecular mechanisms governing protein-protein interactions (PPI) contributing to the biogenesis of membraneless condensates remain poorly understood. Observations reported in this contribution demonstrate that liquid-liquid phase-separated (LLPS) condensate-forming proteins and their variants harboring condensate-disrupting human pathogenic mutations are significantly enriched among PPI cascade constituents of the Hybrid Assembly Pathway (HAP) of endogenous multiprotein complexes (EMC). In contrast, no structured or disordered proteins defined as condensate-negative molecules were detected among PPI cascade constituents. Cumulative evidence derived from different lines of analytical inquiries support the concept of the significant contributions of PPI cascade constituents of HAP to the biogenesis of intracellular pools of cell type-specific protein heterodimers assembled with the engagement of at least one protein monomer selected from condensate-forming protein molecules. Analytical evidence documented in this study support the concept that genome-wide chromosome-naïve networks of transposon-seeded DNA-templates nucleate the initiation of PPI cascade chain reactions with the capacity to facilitate the concurrent iterative assembly in human cells of both the membraneless biomolecular condensates and the structured multiprotein complexes. AlphaFold 3D modelling of mutation patterns and analysis of partner-bound experimental structures identified variants located at a measured protein dimerization interface. This contribution nominates numerous protein heterodimers of potential relevance to human pathophysiology for follow-up mechanistic studies of potential fundamental and translational impacts.

### [2] [D2ML: Models for Direct-to-Biology Small Molecule Screening](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41070)
*AkshatKumar Nigam,Neri Amara,Hillary Dequina,Dyana Kenanova,Seth Malmersjo,Fiorella Ruggiu,Raymond V. Fucini,Jack Sadowsky,Stig K. Hansen,Imran S. Haque*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 通过针对D2B数据结构的模型改进预测，指导发现更强效和选择性化合物。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: D2B筛选能快速测试未纯化分子，但混合物中浓度不确定性和有限纯化数据限制了机器学习应用，需改进模型以优化分子设计。

Method: 开发基于D2B数据的多阶段联合模型，预测单点筛选活性、粗剂量响应相对效力和纯化化合物绝对效力，并通过共享信息提升预测。

Result: 模型显著提升粗剂量响应的选择性预测（排名相关性从0.27升至0.40），实验中发现新化合物ROS1 IC50达14.7 nM，选择性提升233倍。

Conclusion: 结合D2B多阶段数据的模型可有效指导多目标分子优化，显著提升化合物效力和选择性。

Abstract: Direct-to-biology (D2B) screening enables rapid testing of newly synthesized molecules without purification. However, uncertain concentrations in crude mixtures and limited purified-compound measurements complicate the use of these data in machine learning. Here we show that models tailored to the structure of D2B data can improve prediction and guide the discovery of more potent and selective compounds. Our assay-aware models predict activity from single-point screens, relative potency across targets from crude dose-response assays, and absolute potency from purified-compound measurements. For crude assays, potency ratios between targets tested from the same well can retain selectivity information despite uncertain concentrations. A joint model that shares information across stages and balances their contributions to training improved predictions over separate neural networks. The largest gain was in crude dose-response selectivity prediction, where rank correlation increased from 0.27 to 0.40. In a separate experiment, independently trained models guided the selection of 84 new compounds to improve ROS1 potency while limiting activity against the anti-target TRKB. Synthesis and testing identified compounds that extended the measured Pareto front, achieving better combinations of potency and selectivity than those in the training data. The most selective new compound achieved a ROS1 IC50 of 14.7 nM and 233-fold selectivity over TRKB. By comparison, the most selective training compound had a ROS1 IC50 of 20.3 nM and 79-fold selectivity. ATLAS provides the experimental setting for this approach, supporting synthesis and screening of 1,000-10,000 model-selected compounds in two- to three-week cycles. Together, these results show how assay-aware learning can use the full D2B assay sequence to guide multi-objective molecular optimization.
