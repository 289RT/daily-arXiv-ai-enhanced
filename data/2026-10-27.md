### [1] [Agent-guided epitope excision enables economical de novo binder design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33195)
*Jun W. Kim,Artiom Myaskovsky,Oscar Donosa,Lawrence Chan,Jamie Rogers,Avinatan Hassidim,Yossi Matias,Annalisa Pawlosky*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 论文提出了一种截断目标蛋白局部界面窗口的方法，以加速蛋白质设计和结构预测。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 蛋白质设计的计算时间限制了功能性探索，尤其是针对大蛋白如FAP。

Method: 采用截断目标局部界面的方法，结合RFdiffusion和AlphaFold3，并通过自主优化循环提高效率。

Result: 方法显著减少计算时间（88%），提高命中率（从5.06%到9.33%），并成功设计出纳摩尔级结合剂。

Conclusion: 目标截断和自主优化策略为大规模蛋白质设计提供了实用方案。

Abstract: De novo protein design campaigns remain bottlenecked by the inference compute time required to generate and rank candidates, limiting wider functional exploration. Here, we introduce and evaluate an epitope excision approach that truncates targets to local interface windows to accelerate both backbone diffusion and structure prediction. Across 15 targets and 3,666 designs, excised windows closely reproduced full-target binding interfaces (median CαRMSD of 0.37 Å) and made it practical to design binders against the 719-residue extracellular domain of fibroblast activation protein alpha (FAP) by cutting RFdiffusion and AlphaFold3 GPU time by ~ 88% in one of the epitopes. Pairing this workflow with an agentic optimization loop allowed the system to autonomously identify catalytic-site targeting strategies. Under a fixed compute budget, a human-in-the-loop (HITL) workflow raised the computational hit rate from 5.06% to 9.33% while further cutting GPU hours by ~ 42%. We also found that omitting target multiple sequence alignments during refolding roughly substantially increased the apparent yield. Wet-lab validation with yeast display identified nanomolar binders from the generated designs with the HITL and the fully autonomous agent (human-out-of-the-loop, HOOTL) arms recovering more enriched designs than the PI-alone baseline. Together, target excision and agent-guided search offer a practical way to scale de novo binder design to large, therapeutically relevant proteins.

### [2] [MechGate: combining mechanism-derived measures of permeation and efflux to screen small molecule drugs targeting Escherichia coli](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33809)
*Lang Qin,Jagath C Rajapakse*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: MechGate 是一种机制解析的筛选工具，用于评估小分子药物对抗大肠杆菌的渗透、外排和抗菌活性，提高抗生素设计的精确度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 目前的抗生素设计中，评估生成的分子未能区分渗透和外排步骤，限制了设计的有效性。

Method: MechGate 通过五个独立指标评估分子：两种通过孔蛋白渗透、一种通过磷脂膜渗透、一种外排和一种抗菌活性。

Result: 在已上市抗生素的外部数据集上，MechGate 的精确度显著提升至 0.92，远超基线。

Conclusion: MechGate 提供了一个开源且无需商业软件的工具，显著优化了抗生素分子的筛选过程。

Abstract: Most antibiotic molecules must cross the cell membrane and escape efflux pumps before hitting their targets. For de novo antibiotic design, very few of the in-silico-generated drug molecules can successfully pass these two steps. Existing evaluations of generated molecules do not separate these steps. Therefore, we introduce MechGate, a mechanism-resolved screening tool evaluating small drug molecules against E. coli, measuring permeation, efflux and antibacterial activity. After SMILES input, five measures are reported side by side: two for permeation via porins, one for permeation via the phospholipid membrane, one for efflux and one for activity. Each measure is read on its own, so a molecule that fails can be traced to the step that stops it. All features are taken from papers that state their mechanism rather than selected by importance ranking. On an external set of marketed antibacterials the measures give a precision of 0.789 to 0.873 against baselines near 0.44, and combining them the way the mechanism predicts raises precision to 0.92. We also find that the four public source libraries are separable from one another using physicochemical features alone; every measure here therefore draws both classes from a single library. All code is open-source and requires no commercial software.

</details>


### [3] [mdRNAcat: A Molecular Dynamics RNA Catalog of REST2 Conformational Ensembles](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35204)
*Anton E. Dorn,Emile de Bruyn,Fabrice von der Lehr,Philipp Knechtges,Stefan Kesselheim,Alexander Schug*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: mdRNAcat是一个精心策划的RNA分子动力学模拟数据集，包含48个多样化RNA系统的模拟数据，用于提高数据驱动RNA模型的开发和评估。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 学习RNA的分子模型需要能够捕捉构象变异并提供一致物理训练信号的模拟数据。

Method: 数据集包含48个RNA系统的全原子MD模拟，采用REST2方法，每个系统模拟32个哈密顿状态，每个状态持续200-300 ns。数据集提供原子坐标、力和结构注释。

Result: 数据集记录了构象采样、交换行为、碱基配对和糖环构型分布，为开发数据驱动的RNA模型提供了资源。

Conclusion: mdRNAcat是一个可重用的数据集，支持RNA模型的开发与评估，包括粗粒化力场。

Abstract: Learning molecular models of RNA requires simulation data that capture conformational variability and provide consistent physical training signals. Here, we introduce mdRNAcat, a curated dataset of all-atom molecular dynamics (MD) simulations of 48 RNA systems selected to promote sequence diversity. Each system is simulated using replica-exchange with solute scaling (REST2), with 32 Hamiltonian states and production lengths of 200-300 ns per state. The dataset provides atomic coordinates, instantaneous forces, and forces reevaluated under the unscaled reference Hamiltonian, together with structural annotations and replica-exchange metadata. Coordinates and forces are available at 1 ps intervals for the reference state and 100 ps intervals across all states in a standardized HDF5 format. We characterize conformational sampling, exchange behavior, base pairing, and sugar-pucker distributions to document the properties of the simulated ensembles. mdRNAcat provides a reusable resource for developing and evaluating data-driven RNA models, including coarse-grained force fields.

</details>
