### [1] [Structural Agreement Indicates Prediction Accuracy For Protein-Ligand Co-Folding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27058)
*J. Henry Broster,Bojana Popovic,Diana Kondinskaia,Charlotte M. Deane,Fergus Imrie*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 研究发现现有共折叠模型的置信度评分对预测准确性提升有限，提出新的CORE评分方法，基于结构一致性更有效预测可靠性。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 评估共折叠模型的置信度评分是否可靠，并提出更有效的可靠性指标。

Method: 分析七个共折叠模型的预测结果，发现结构一致性优于置信度评分，提出CORE评分。

Result: CORE评分在区分正确与错误预测上表现优于现有方法（ROC AUC 0.866 vs. 0.757）。

Conclusion: 结构一致性是更实用的可靠性指标，CORE评分为未来置信度评估设定了基线。

Abstract: Co-folding models predict the structures of proteins and their bound ligands, but their accuracy varies across targets. Their usefulness therefore depends on knowing which predictions a user can trust. Confidence scores are intended to guide that choice. However, across seven co-folding models, we show that confidence-based selection offers little improvement over choosing a prediction at random. For approximately three-quarters of structures, predictions are either exclusively correct or incorrect, leaving no opportunity for ranking to change the outcome. Moreover, high confidence does not reliably discriminate correct top-ranked predictions from incorrect ones. We find that structural agreement among predictions is a more accurate measure of likely success than current confidence scores. We capture this with the Concentration of Repeated Ensembles (CORE) Score, which combines cluster tightness with ensemble support. CORE Score discriminates top-1 success and oracle success, achieving mean ROC AUCs of 0.866 and 0.838, compared to 0.757 and 0.759 for ipTM. CORE Score also outperforms training set similarity measures, including ligand Tanimoto, protein sequence, and Runs N' Poses similarity, despite requiring no experimental ligand pose. Structural agreement is therefore a practical indicator of reliability and a baseline for evaluating future confidence estimators, without requiring an additional trained uncertainty estimator.

### [2] [Hierarchical Hybrid-Coordinate Flow Matching for Accurate and Physically Plausible Apo-to-Holo Protein Generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27162)
*Minjong Kim,Jaemin Sim,Dongwoo Kim,Myunghyun Jang,Eun-Sol Kim,Juyong Lee*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: CrypticFlow是一种分层流匹配模型，结合了笛卡尔和内部坐标表示，用于从apo结构预测holo蛋白质构象。该方法在全局和局部几何精度上均优于现有基线。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 在基于结构的药物设计中，从apo结构预测holo蛋白质构象至关重要。现有模型在全局精度上表现良好，但局部几何结构常不满足物理有效性。CrypticFlow旨在结合两种互补表示来解决这一问题。

Method: CrypticFlow采用分层流匹配模型：先用ESM-3条件和E(3)等变图网络生成稀疏Cα锚点，然后用条件中点Transformer递归填充局部内部坐标。生成过程中通过约束距离保持局部几何有效性。

Result: 在D3PM测试集上，CrypticFlow的中值和平均Cα RMSD均为最低（1.333和2.766埃）。局部几何质量显著提升（相邻距离和伪角保真度分别为99.58%和95.87%），MolProbity评分为3.034。

Conclusion: 分层流匹配模型结合混合坐标可在保持全局精度的同时提升局部保真度。CrypticFlow为蛋白质构象预测提供了新解决方案。

Abstract: Predicting holo-like protein conformations from apo structures is essential in structure-based drug design. Most existing apo-to-holo models operate in Cartesian space and achieve strong global accuracy. However, high global accuracy does not ensure physically valid local backbone geometry. Internal-coordinate models offer local control and validity, but regressive generation can amplify angular errors, known as the lever-arm effect. We introduce CrypticFlow, a hierarchical flow-matching model that combines these two complementary representations. An ESM-3-conditioned E(3)-equivariant graph network first generates sparse C$α$ anchors in Cartesian space. A conditional midpoint Transformer then recursively fills the gaps in local internal coordinates. During recursive generation, the decoder constrains the distance between each midpoint and any sequence-adjacent parent anchor to the corresponding apo C$α$-C$α$ distance. On the D3PM test split, CrypticFlow achieves the lowest median and mean C$α$ RMSD at published baseline capacities. Across five independent training models, the median and mean direct RMSD are 1.333 $\pm$ 0.056 angstroms and 2.766 $\pm$ 0.041 angstroms, respectively. The quality of local geometry also improves substantially. Our model achieves 99.58 $\pm$ 0.02% adjacent-distance fidelity and 95.87 $\pm$ 0.25% pseudo-angle fidelity, measured within 0.2 angstroms and 20° of the holo reference. Then, we reconstruct the predicted C$α$-only traces into all-atom structures using the cg2all coarse-to-all-atom backmapping method and evaluate their physical and geometric quality with MolProbity. Our model achieves a MolProbity score of 3.034 $\pm$ 0.054, outperforming the evaluated baselines. These results show that a hierarchical flow-matching model with hybrid coordinates can recover global accuracy while retaining local fidelity.

### [3] [BioEmu2: Scalable Emulation of Biomolecular Dynamics](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27924)
*Michael Gastegger,Adam E. Foster,José Jiménez-Luna,Tim Hempel,Sarah Lewis,Yu Xie,Lixin Sun,Iryna Zaporozhets,J. Thorben Frank,Yaoyi Chen,Michael Plainer,Ludwig Winkler,Pedro B. P. S. Reis,Sven H. Giese,Roberto Sordillo,Victor García Satorras,Eray Inanc,Kacper Kapuśniak,Soojung Yang,Rishwanth Raghu,Rachel Kim,Gabriel Dernbach,Deniz Gunceler,Hannes Schulz,Bonnie Kruft,Marwin Segler,Christopher M. Bishop,Cecilia Clementi,Ryota Tomioka,Frank Noé*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: BioEmu2是一种生成模型，结合静态结构预测、平衡采样和时间演化，能够快速生成高分辨率生物分子结构，适用于蛋白质、核酸等多种生物分子系统。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 尽管结构预测取得了重大进展，但在宏观尺度上模拟生物分子运动与相互作用仍然是一个核心挑战。BioEmu2旨在解决这一问题。

Method: BioEmu2基于实验和合成数据以及大量分子动力学数据训练，支持多组分系统。其结构预测准确度可比顶级生物分子折叠模型，并能预测超大复合物。

Result: BioEmu2的平衡分布接近分子动力学参考结果，自由能误差约为1 kcal/mol；时间传播可预测长时间尺度的动力学轨迹和结合/构象变化。

Conclusion: BioEmu2通过增强采样和用户控制，使生物分子集合与动力学研究更具可扩展性。

Abstract: Despite major advances in structure prediction, modelling the motions and interactions that drive biomolecular function at scale remains a central challenge. We introduce BioEmu2, a generative model for atomically resolved biomolecular structures combining static structure prediction, equilibrium sampling and time evolution, that samples orders of magnitude faster than molecular dynamics (MD). Trained on experimental and synthetic structures and extensive MD data, it supports multi-component systems containing proteins, nucleic acids, modified residues, ligands and ions. Structure-prediction accuracy is comparable to top open-weight biomolecular folding models, and a context parallel mode enables prediction of megadalton complexes. Equilibrium distributions approximate MD references with free-energy errors near 1 kcal/mol across tested proteins and complexes. Time propagation predicts long-timescale dynamical trajectories and kinetics of binding and conformational changes. Generated structures can be controlled by conditioning on residue contacts or steering with user-defined bias potentials. Enhanced sampling enables free energy calculation involving rare states. BioEmu2 makes the study of biomolecular ensembles and dynamics scalable.
