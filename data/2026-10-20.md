### [1] [LiveCoMS Roundtable: Discussing Markov state models after two decades of development v1.0](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24144)
*Daniel M. Zuckerman,Gregory R. Bowman,Amedeo Caflisch,Aaron R. Dinner,Ahmed Fadhluddin,Giuseppe Giangreco,Xuhui Huang,Bettina G. Keller,Stefan Klus,Feliks Nüske,Michael O'Connor,Edina Rosta,Christof Schütte,Gerhard Stock,Vincent Voelz,Jonathan Weare,Antonia S. J. S. Mey*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 该论文总结了Markov状态模型（MSM）在分子动力学模拟中的应用及其发展，收集了多位专家的不同观点，未达成共识，旨在提供多样化的专家视角。

Motivation: 探讨MSM在分子动力学模拟中的发展现状、局限性及改进方向，汇总专家意见以帮助读者理解该领域的多样化视角。

Abstract: Markov state models (MSMs) play a central role in the analysis of molecular dynamics (MD) simulations for biomolecules, and MSM methodologies have been under continual development since their introduction to the field in the early 2000s. MSMs take MD trajectory data as input and produce a more compact, i.e., coarse-grained, and interpretable representation of the system in terms of conformational states and quantitative observables such as the equilibrium populations of states and the timescales of interconverting between states. Numerous strategies and considerable mathematical formalism have been developed to improve coarse-graining in both time and space to enable the most reliable representation of the underlying biomolecular dynamics. Nevertheless, there remains considerable discussion in the literature regarding best practices and MSM limitations, as well as proposals to abandon or extend the first-order Markovian framework in common use. This Roundtable article gathers together diverse opinions from $> 10$ authors active in MSM development, and by design, each author or author group offers a separate contribution. No consensus view is provided. Instead, the article is meant to be a guide for readers wishing to be exposed to a wide set of expert perspectives.

### [2] [Aptamers as a sandbox for lab-in-the-loop agentic molecular discovery](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.24806)
*Zachary Cutts,John Coller,Rachel Chen,Hua Tang,Mathias Bellaiche,Ryder Easterlin,Dina Zverinski,Petar Sirkovic,Avinatan Hassidim,Yossi Matias,Pooja Rao,Annalisa Pawlosky*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: AI驱动的分子生成与湿实验室数据闭环迭代显著提升了蛋白质结合适配体的设计效率，展示了AI代理在实验室反馈中逐步改进的能力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探索大型语言模型（LLM）代理能否通过与实验室数据的迭代加速分子发现，弥补现有计算方法的不足。

Method: 以蛋白质结合适配体设计为实验场景，通过多个设计-测试-改进循环（每个循环约1000个候选分子），结合湿实验室数据闭环优化。

Abstract: Despite breakthroughs in AI-driven molecular generation, existing discovery pipelines operate largely in silico, with wet-lab feedback reserved for validation rather than iterative improvement. Whether large language model (LLM)-based reasoning agents can accelerate de novo discovery by iterating with lab data remains unproven.
  Here we use protein-binding aptamer design as a sandbox (1-2 week cycles, \$1-\$3 per candidate) to demonstrate a wet lab in a closed loop with AI systems. We tasked agentic pipelines with developing aptamer binders against $α$-thrombin and myoglobin across three design-test-improve cycles ($\sim$1,000 candidates per round; $\sim$4,400 constructs). Statistically significant, bead-corrected on-target hit rates among new candidates rose across rounds from 0.0% in Round 1 to 26.0% in Round 2 and 42.1% in Round 3 on $α$-thrombin (37.1% excluding literature motif descendants), and from 10.2% in Round 1 to 3.7% in Round 2 and 33.5% in Round 3 on myoglobin. Single-clone bead-binding titrations of eight full-length $α$-thrombin candidates confirmed concordance with pooled sequencing enrichment (Pearson $r = 0.90$). In these assays, literature-derived motifs engineered by the agents to reduce off-target reactivity in the pooled screen reached apparent affinities of $K_D = 24.6$--$133$ nM, whereas de novo scaffolds showed low-micromolar binding ($K_D > 1\ μ$M).
  We also report how our pipelines used myoglobin binding data to exploit the G-quadruplex binder space, diagnose cross-reactivity as on-target specificity dropped from 75.0% to 19.1%, and recover by developing strategies that restored specificity to 72.4%. We demonstrate that empirical lab data is more valuable than existing computational proxies, showing how iterative experimental feedback enables AI agents to diagnose assay artifacts and refine molecular designs across rounds.

### [3] [Accurate quantification of protein concentrations and state populations by 2D 1H-N Q-BEST NMR](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26017)
*Yacine Ouali,Beate Bersch,Bernhard Brutscher*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 2D Q-BEST NMR是一种定量方法，用于测定蛋白质浓度和相对状态分布，具有高灵敏度和短实验时间的特点。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 希望通过一种高效且精确的方法来定量分析蛋白质的浓度和构象状态分布。

Method: 利用BEST技术，通过一组三个1H-15N相关光谱进行定量分析。

Result: 在泛素模型系统中验证了方法的有效性，单个交叉峰的精度约为15-20%，且可通过平均校正提高精度。

Conclusion: 2D Q-BEST NMR是一种准确且可靠的定量生物分子NMR分析工具。

Abstract: We introduce 2D Q-BEST NMR as a quantitative approach for determining protein concentrations and relative state populations from a set of three 1 H-15 N correlation spectra. The BEST methodology ensures short overall experimental times and highest sensitivity. The method was validated using ubiquitin as a model system, where a precision of approximately 15-20% was achieved at the level of individual cross-peaks. This precision can be further improved by averaging corrected intensities of well-resolved resonances corresponding to the same protein state. The approach was subsequently applied to the quantification of protein concentrations across multiple samples and to the determination of conformational state populations in proteins exhibiting intrinsic or ligand-induced heterogeneity. In both cases, 2D Q-BEST NMR provides accurate and robust quantification of NMR-visible species, demonstrating its utility as a general spectroscopic tool for quantitative biomolecular NMR analysis.

### [4] [ProteinGeoBench: Frontier Structural-Biology Agents Are Fluent at Lookup but Lost in Geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26829)
*Matt Turk,Brandon L. Neel,Diana M. Lopez,Zachary D. Smith,Grace N. Johnecheck,James B. Golden*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 该论文研究了蛋白质结构任务的评估配置对前沿模型性能的影响，发现模型间的性能差异和评估配置密切相关。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究蛋白质结构任务中前沿模型的性能差异如何受到评估配置的影响，以提供更客观的评估方法。

Method: 使用ProteinGeoBench套件中的131个蛋白质结构任务，分析了4,249次前沿模型测试和4,486次其他模型测试的数据。

Result: 前沿模型间性能差异不显著，但评估配置（如运行次数、任务子集等）对排名有较大影响。

Conclusion: 建议评估时应报告任务族别的结果，并详细说明运行次数、评估框架和访问条件。

Abstract: Frontier agents retrieve structural metadata well and compute geometry from coordinates less reliably. We ask how much of a benchmark score reflects that gap and how much reflects the evaluation configuration around the model. We study ProteinGeoBench, a suite of 131 protein-structure tasks, using 4,249 rollouts from four frontier models and 4,486 further trials across 26 models. Three of the four frontier models fall within 0.031 of one another (0.785 to 0.816) with no difference between them resolved, while the fourth scores 0.707 and all three gaps to it exclude zero. Three of the four do better on metadata lookup than on numeric calculation. The fourth is slightly better on numeric. Numeric tasks are 28 of the 131 but half of the twelve tasks that all four models fail. The ranking also depends on the evaluation budget: with one run per task, 23.9% of bootstrap replicates put a different model first. On a selected 15-task slice, removing network access lowers scores more than blocking eight structural-database domains, but the two contrasts were graded in different passes and cannot be cleanly decomposed. Swapping in each model's own command-line agent changes scores by -0.071 to +0.086, a comparison too small to rule out moderate harness effects. Across 26 models, numeric performance rises with non-numeric performance rather than staying flat. The experiments use different task subsets, so they cannot rank model, harness, access and task composition against one another. Evaluations should report results by task family, along with the number of runs, the harness and the access conditions.

</details>
