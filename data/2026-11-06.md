### [1] [Kinetic models of a K channel with 2 stage voltage sensor activation and an opening step](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08354)
*S. R. Vaccaro*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 该论文探讨了钾通道激活的动力学模型，从4状态简化到3状态，并进一步分析了10状态模型的开放概率与电压依赖性速率的关系。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究钾通道在不同状态下的动力学行为，简化复杂模型以更好地理解其激活机制。

Method: 通过假设速率条件简化4状态模型为3状态，并扩展为10状态模型来分析开放概率和电压依赖性速率。

Result: 在特定条件下，开放状态概率与n²成正比，且派生速率与电压钳数据中的α_n和β_n具有相似的电压依赖性。

Conclusion: 该研究为钾通道的动力学行为提供了简化和扩展模型，有助于理解其激活机制与电压依赖性的关系。

Abstract: The activation of a K channel with 2 voltage sensors and a separate opening step may be described by a 4 state kinetic equation. If $k_{o}$ and $k_{c}$ are the opening and closing rates, and $α$ and $β$ are the voltage sensor rates such that $k_{o}$ $\ggα,k_{c}\ggβ$ and $k_{o}$ $\gg k_{c}$, the kinetic equation may be reduced to a 3 state model where the effective backward rate from the activated-open state is $2βk_{c}/(k_{o}+k_{c})$. However, if a K channel voltage sensor activates in 2 stages, and there is a separate opening step, the occupation probabilities of the states of the channel satisfy a 10 state kinetic equation. When the time constants of the characteristic equation for each voltage sensor differ by an order of magnitude, and $k_{o}$ $\gg k_{c}$, the open state probability is proportional to $n^{2}$, where $n$ satisfies a rate equation, and the derived rates have a similar voltage dependence to the rates $α_{n}$ and $β_{n}$ from voltage clamp data.

### [2] [Static Recovery Is Not Dynamic Stability: Dynamics-Aware Benchmarking of Protein Motif Scaffolding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07723)
*Emile de Bruyn,Moritz Schäffler,Jannik Schneider,Sebastian R. Schmidt,Chanwoong Hwang,Aleena Siji,Utkarsh Upadhyay,Stefan Bauer,Alexander Schug,Stefan Kesselheim,Artur Yakimovich,Alina Bazarova*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: BILBY-PRO和BILBY-MD是两个新的蛋白质支架生成模型评估框架，分别专注于系统化功能基序的静态重建和动态保真度验证。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有蛋白质基序支架生成模型的评估存在两个问题：依赖小规模手工选取的基序和静态折叠标准，而蛋白质功能通常依赖于构象集合和动态变化。因此，需要更系统化和动态化的评估方法。

Method: 1. 提出BILBY-PRO，从PROSITE数据库系统化提取220个功能基序案例，评估六种模型的覆盖率和独特解生成能力。2. 提出BILBY-MD，通过全原子分子动力学模拟验证静态设计结果的动态保真度，包括基序漂移、支架漂移、接触保持和自由能景观等指标。

Result: BILBY-PRO测试显示RFDiffusion和Proteina能解决更多目标，而ESM3在解决的目标上产生更多独特支架。BILBY-MD发现55个静态解中仅31个满足动态标准，8个通过全部检查。紧凑独立基序动态表现更好，界面或上下文依赖基序易失败。

Conclusion: 静态重构成功可作为局部基序恢复的证据，但不能保证支架的整体动态保真度。BILBY框架强调评估的三个互补维度：目标覆盖率、独特解生成和动态保真度。

Abstract: Protein motif scaffolding benchmarks assess whether generative models can build scaffolds around functionally important residues. Their conclusions depend on which motifs are tested and which criteria define success. Existing benchmarks typically use small hand-curated motif sets and static refolding criteria, but protein function often depends on conformational ensembles and transitions. Here, we address both benchmark scope and success criteria. We introduce BILBY-PRO, a systematically derived benchmark of structurally conserved functional motifs from PROSITE-linked experimental structures, comprising 220 cases from 174 PROSITE motif-pattern entries. Evaluating six recent scaffold-generation models reveals distinct patterns in target coverage and unique-solution yield: RFDiffusion and Proteina solve more targets, whereas ESM3 produces more unique successful scaffolds on the targets it solves. We further introduce BILBY-MD, a reference-based all-atom molecular dynamics evaluation framework. Applied to statically solved designs from the original MotifBench benchmark, it compares motif drift, scaffold drift, motif-contact preservation, and motif-dihedral free-energy landscapes against reference simulations. Of 55 statically solved model-problem pairs, 31 satisfy our MD-supported criterion and only 8 pass all checks. Compact, self-contained motifs are often MD-supported, whereas interface-like or context-dependent motifs fail more frequently. Static refolding success therefore provides useful evidence of local motif recovery but does not establish dynamical fidelity of the full scaffold. BILBY highlights three complementary axes of motif-scaffolding evaluation: target coverage, unique-solution yield, and MD-supported dynamical fidelity.

### [3] [Geometric Relevance Is Not Sufficient: Evaluating Focused Post-Training Supervision in Diffusion Docking](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08249)
*Michael Brown,Maryam Rahimimovassagh,Mehdi Yazdani-Jahromi,Sanjeda Sara Jennifer,Ivan Garibay*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 研究比较了扩散分子对接中不同监督策略的效果，发现广泛的持续性监督在Top-1成功率上表现最佳，而聚焦或混合监督在测试预算下未显示出明显优势。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探讨扩散分子对接中不同的监督策略（广泛的、聚焦的或混合的）对生成姿态质量的影响。

Method: 使用DiffDock进行固定预算研究，比较广泛的持续性监督、聚焦于结构后果的监督以及混合监督策略。

Result: 广泛的持续性监督在Top-1成功率（<5 Angstrom）上表现最佳，而其他监督策略未见明显优势。

Conclusion: 在有限预算下，广泛的持续性监督是更优选择，而聚焦监督未显著提升对接成功率。

Abstract: Diffusion-based molecular docking requires learning a corrective score field across a broad corruption distribution, yet individual pose errors can differ substantially in their structural consequences. This creates a post-training conflict between preserving broad score-field coverage and concentrating supervision on corrections that appear more relevant to docking geometry. Using DiffDock, a fixed-budget study compares broad continuation with torsional supervision focused on Cartesian consequence or conditional target consistency, and with a broad/focused mixture. Evaluation separates fixed score fidelity, generated-pose quality, best-of-10 sampling capacity, and confidence-selected Top-1 docking performance. At <5 Angstrom, broad continuation had the highest mean Top-1 success among trained policies, and focused or mixed supervision showed no consistent advantage under the tested budget. The starting model had better fixed score fidelity, whereas the evaluated broad-continuation model had higher mean Top-1 success with the same aggregate best-of-10 recovery. Focusing supervision on structurally consequential or more consistently specified corrections did not consistently improve returned-pose success over broad continuation.

</details>
