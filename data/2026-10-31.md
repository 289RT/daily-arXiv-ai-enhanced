### [1] [AI-driven structural informatics reveals diverse glycosylation machinery across double-stranded DNA viruses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39662)
*Lan Na*

Main category: q-bio.BM

TL;DR: 该研究通过结合结构相似性网络和基因组邻域分析，揭示了病毒GT-A蛋白的广泛结构多样性，并发现了一种新型病毒DNA修饰形式及其潜在生物功能。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 旨在更全面地理解病毒糖基转移酶（GTs）的多样性和生物合成组织，尤其是那些被序列注释忽略的结构多样性。

Method: 采用结构字母引导的搜索、结构相似性网络、基因组邻域分析和生物合成基因簇嵌入分析，对病毒GT-A蛋白进行研究。

Result: 发现了未被描述的病毒GT谱系，并鉴定出一个巨型噬菌体位点，该位点编码五种串联糖基转移酶，表明存在一种涉及硫酸化五糖的病毒DNA修饰形式。

Conclusion: 研究揭示了病毒糖基转移酶的丰富多样性及其在多种分子背景和生物功能中的潜在作用，扩展了对病毒糖基化生物合成的认识。

Abstract: Glycosylation is widespread in biology and shapes molecular recognition, yet the diversity and biosynthetic organization of viral glycosyltransferases (GTs) remain poorly understood. Here we integrate structural-alphabet-guided searches and structure similarity networks with genomic-neighbourhood and biosynthetic gene cluster embedding analyses to examine viral GT-A proteins. This approach reveals extensive structural diversity overlooked by sequence-based annotation, including previously uncharacterized viral GT lineages. Most strikingly, we identified a jumbo-phage locus encoding five tandem glycosyltransferases alongside hydroxymethyl-pyrimidine biosynthetic and sulfation-associated enzymes, suggesting a previously unrecognized form of viral DNA modification involving a sulfated pentasaccharide. Beyond these examples, predicted saccharide-associated loci span divergent double-stranded DNA viruses, from giant viruses to tailed phages, and occupy multiple regions of the reference biosynthetic landscape. Together, our findings uncover an underappreciated repertoire of virus-encoded glycosylation machinery and suggest that viral glycan biosynthesis encompasses diverse molecular contexts and potential biological functions.

### [2] [What to randomize matters: a controlled study of volume, axis and representation in low-data molecular generation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38835)
*Neerav Kaushal,Alisa Teige,Ajay MNV Penmatsa*

Main category: cs.LG

TL;DR: 研究比较了SAFE随机化和SMILES随机化在少量数据下生成新分子的效果，发现SAFE随机化显著提高生成新分子的能力。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 化学语言模型在小数据集上训练时容易复现训练样本，而非生成新分子，研究比较了两种随机化方法的效果。

Method: 使用1000个种子分子训练自回归序列模型，比较标准SAFE与随机SAFE，并分析原子序和片段序的影响。

Result: SAFE随机化使新分子生成量提升450倍，两种随机化轴共同作用效果显著优于单独作用，随机SAFE效果优于随机SMILES。

Conclusion: 在小数据集下，随机化的方式而非数量决定了生成新分子的能力。

Abstract: Chemical language models can struggle to generate new molecules when trained on small datasets, where a model may reproduce training examples rather than learn a useful generative distribution. Randomized SMILES augmentation varies atom traversal, whereas Sequential Attachment-based Fragment Embedding (SAFE) also allows the order of fragment blocks to vary. How these distinct sources of representational variation affect low-data molecular generation has not been clearly studied. Here, using 1,000 seed molecules to train an autoregressive sequence model, we compare canonical SAFE with randomized SAFE at increasing corpus sizes, isolate atom-order and fragment-order axes of SAFE randomization, and compare fully randomized SAFE with randomized SMILES at molecule-by-molecule matched training corpora. Three findings emerge. First, randomization of SAFE alone raises the genuine yield of novel molecules more than 450-fold, turning a model that merely reproduces its training set into a practical molecular generator. Second, the two axes of randomization act jointly rather than additively: atom order alone reaches a tenth, and fragment order alone a seventh, of the genuine yield obtained when both vary, which is four times their combined contribution. Third, at matched corpus size and compute, randomized SAFE delivers 2.8 times the genuine yield of randomized SMILES. Welch tests with Games-Howell post-hoc comparisons resolve every difference on the primary outcomes. In the low-data regime, thus, what one randomizes rather than only how much determines whether augmentation produces new molecules or merely new spellings of the training set.

</details>
